Syria

May 9, 2026 · Government High impact

Sharaa runs the first cabinet reshuffle since Assad's ouster, moves brother Maher out of the secretary-general post and rotates four governors

Late-night presidential decrees on Saturday named former Homs governor Abdulrahman Badreddine al-A'ma as secretary-general of the presidency in place of the president's brother Maher al-Sharaa, installed Khaled Fawaz Zaarour as Information Minister and rotated outgoing minister Hamza al-Mustafa to the Foreign Ministry, named Bassel Hafez al-Sweidan as Agriculture Minister, and replaced governors of Homs, Latakia, Deir Ezzor and Quneitra, with Decree 102 putting Ghassan Elias al-Sayed Ahmad over Quneitra. The reshuffle is the first since Assad's December 2024 fall and lands roughly 18 months into the five-year transitional period set out in the constitutional declaration, against a backdrop of social-media campaigns over economic conditions and government performance.

May 9, 2026 · Diplomacy

Lebanese PM Salam books significant Damascus progress with Sharaa on borders, prisoners and energy

Lebanese Prime Minister Nawaf Salam met President Ahmad al-Sharaa at the People's Palace on Saturday in a rare top-level Beirut visit to Damascus, accompanied by Deputy Prime Minister Tarek Mitri and the energy, economy and transport ministers, with Salam describing significant progress on stricter Syria-Lebanon border controls and anti-smuggling, the more than 2,000 Syrians held in Lebanese jails and the missing in both countries, and a deeper economy and trade cooperation track. The session follows the March transfer of more than 130 Syrian prisoners from Lebanon and slots into the post-Assad rapprochement that Beirut and Damascus have been rebuilding since the 18-month transition started.

IDF artillery and ground raids hit Quneitra's Koudna and Tell Ahmar al-Sharqi as Jordanian airstrikes return over Suwayda

Israeli forces raided villages in Quneitra countryside on Saturday, opening heavy fire and shelling forest areas near Koudna and Tell Ahmar al-Sharqi with artillery, while Jordanian airstrikes were carried out in the Suwayda countryside in parallel. Iraq

May 8, 2026 · Sanctions

Iraq's Oil Ministry rejects the US Treasury accusations on deputy minister al-Bahadli, says crude export operations were not in his job

Iraq's Oil Ministry late Thursday denied the OFAC accusations against deputy minister Ali Maarij al-Bahadli, called for evidence-based handling and pledged to investigate, while stressing that crude oil export operations, marketing, loading onto tankers and related procedures were not part of al-Bahadli's job description. The pushback follows the Treasury's May 7 designation that named al-Bahadli, two Iran-linked oil-smuggling networks and three KSS and AAH leaders, and lands at the same time several Iraqi legislators and media reports were flagging al-Bahadli as a frontrunner for the Oil Ministry portfolio in al-Zaidi's cabinet.

May 9, 2026 · Banking

Central Bank of Kuwait warns the public against fake trading platforms targeting depositors over social media

The Central Bank of Kuwait issued a public warning on Friday against fraudulent online trading and investment platforms that lure victims via social media and messaging channels, often using anonymous accounts to circulate small sums before pulling larger transfers, and reminded depositors that unlicensed foreign-platform trading carries no legal cover. The advisory builds on the CBK's standing scam-and-fraud guidance and lands as banks, brokerages and KIB-side digital platforms run their own awareness pushes against phishing and smishing campaigns.
