Syria

June 20, 2026 · Politics

Al-Sharaa rules out sending Syrian forces against Hezbollah and shelves the Lebanon border file after Trump again floated a Syrian role

President Ahmed al-Sharaa firmly rejected the idea that Damascus would intervene militarily in Lebanon or move against Hezbollah, after US President Donald Trump publicly suggested Syrian forces could do the job better than Israel, saying Syria seeks to stop the war in Lebanon rather than join it. Al-Sharaa added that Syria and Lebanon have agreed to postpone demarcation of their disputed border to focus on economic cooperation and the return of roughly 1.4 million displaced Syrians still in Lebanon, drawing a clear line under a proposal that had unsettled Beirut.

Asharq Al-Awsat
June 17, 2026 · Humanitarian

New FAO and WFP report keeps Syria on the list of the world's 13 hunger hotspots, warning food insecurity will deepen through November

The latest FAO and WFP Hunger Hotspots early-warning report lists Syria among 13 countries where acute food insecurity is set to worsen between June and November 2026, alongside Sudan, South Sudan, Yemen, and the Gaza Strip at the most severe end. The warning lands as Damascus cuts in-kind support, with the UN estimating about 9.1 million Syrians food insecure and roughly nine in ten people living below the poverty line, underscoring how fragile the recovery remains beneath the political headlines.

Iraq

June 20, 2026 · Energy

Iraq asks Turkey for a one-year extension of the Kirkuk-Ceyhan pipeline treaty as the July 27 expiry closes in with no replacement deal

Baghdad has formally asked Ankara to extend the existing Iraq-Turkey pipeline agreement by at least one year, buying time to negotiate a replacement before the 1973 treaty governing crude exports to Ceyhan lapses on July 27 after Turkey triggered its exit clause. The request is the clearest move yet to keep the northern export route legally open, and it directly shapes the Kurdistan Region's primary oil lifeline, which is still recovering from wartime disruption that cut KRG flows to as little as 20,000 to 40,000 barrels a day.

Egypt Oil & Gas
June 18, 2026 · Energy

Iraq signs a sweeping US energy realignment, reviving the dormant Kirkuk-Baniyas pipeline and adding a southern LNG import terminal

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi and US special envoy Tom Barrack finalized a package of agreements steering Iraq's energy and grid investment toward American firms, including a memorandum with TI Capital to rehabilitate the long-dormant Kirkuk-Baniyas pipeline to Syria's Mediterranean coast, a project estimated at 4 to 5 billion dollars over two to three years. The deals, which also hand Texas-based Excelerate Energy a contract for a floating LNG import terminal at a southern port, are pitched as a way to cut Iraq's reliance on Iranian gas and shore up a power sector battered by the war.

Iraqi News
June 21, 2026 · Politics

Baghdad targets early July to complete the cabinet, filling the defense and interior posts before al-Zaidi's Washington trip

Government spokesman Haidar al-Aboudi said Iraq expects to finish forming its cabinet in early July, ahead of a planned trip by Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi to the United States, more than a month after parliament approved only 14 of 23 ministers on May 14. The nine still-vacant portfolios include the security-critical defense and interior ministries plus higher education and planning, all held up by disputes over candidates and quota shares as the new government tries to project control of the arms file.

Kuwait

June 22, 2026 · Energy

Kuwait offers refined products and crude for pickup from inside the Gulf, the clearest sign yet that Hormuz shipping has normalized

Kuwait Petroleum Corporation is now asking buyers to collect cargoes from its ports deep inside the Gulf, issuing a tender to sell naphtha for pickup at Kuwaiti terminals as traffic through the Strait of Hormuz rebuilds. Kuwait and Abu Dhabi's ADNOC have both floated crude with the option of loading from inside or outside the strait, a shift back to ordinary commercial terms that signals war-risk premiums easing barely two weeks after force majeure was lifted.

Bloomberg
June 19, 2026 · Energy

Kuwait's first post-war crude cargoes load for Asia as KPC issues its July delivery tender in VLCC-sized parcels

KPC issued its July crude delivery tender within hours of lifting all wartime force majeure on June 19, offering 2-million-barrel parcels on delivered-ex-ship terms aimed at large Asian refiners as production climbs back toward 2 million barrels a day. Transitional June cargoes of roughly 4 million barrels were loaded via ship-to-ship transfer off Sohar onto VLCCs that routed to China, the first concrete sign that Kuwaiti crude is physically moving to market again after exports collapsed about 78 percent in May.

Kurdistan

June 21, 2026 · Politics

Nechirvan Barzani lays out an Erbil-Baghdad roadmap at a Sulaimani officer graduation, tying the region's security to a stable federal Iraq

Kurdistan Region President Nechirvan Barzani used the graduation of 210 officers from the 3rd Military College in Qachaghulan near Sulaimani on June 21 to set out a roadmap for Erbil-Baghdad relations, arguing the region's security is bound up with the stability of federal Iraq and offering cooperation to Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's government. He told the new corps to keep the Peshmerga out of partisan politics and reminded Baghdad of its constitutional duty to fund the force, framing a professional, unified Peshmerga as the guarantee of the region's status.

Iraqi News
June 21, 2026 · Culture

Erbil breaks ground on the vast Barzani Grand Mosque, a 2-million-square-meter complex with a 9,000-worshipper prayer hall

KRG Endowments and Religious Affairs Minister Pishtiwan Sadiq laid the foundation stone for the Barzani Grand Mosque in Erbil on June 21 alongside Prime Minister Masrour Barzani and senior clerics, on a site spanning more than 2 million square meters with a main prayer hall built on an artificial hill for about 9,000 worshippers. Billed as a major religious and cultural landmark, the design touts a record-size dome, four minarets with elevators, a large public park, and extensive parking, a marquee Erbil project moving ahead even as the tenth KRG cabinet stays unformed.
