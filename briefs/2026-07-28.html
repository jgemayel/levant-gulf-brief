Syria

July 26, 2026 · Diplomacy

Al-Sharaa says Syria is working toward a security agreement with Israel

In an Al Jazeera interview President Ahmed al-Sharaa said Damascus is pursuing a security agreement with Israel that could open the way to a broader peace without surrendering Syria's claim to the Golan Heights. He said Syria is trying to bring in a large group of countries to pressure Israel toward a more balanced posture, even as Israeli troops keep pushing across the 1974 disengagement line in the south.

Al Jazeera
July 27, 2026 · Politics

Al-Sharaa charts a new foreign policy and moves to trim Russia's bases

Al-Sharaa laid out a foreign policy built on reconstruction, balanced relations and support for Lebanese sovereignty with no Syrian intervention there, saying the country is regaining a regional role after decades of isolation. He said contacts with Moscow have produced an agreement to limit Russia's military footprint at Hmeimim air base and part of the Tartous naval facility, with the future of both sites still under discussion.

July 26, 2026 · Security

Israeli forces set up a checkpoint at Al-Ardah as Quneitra raids continue

Israeli soldiers advanced toward villages in southwestern Syria, set up a checkpoint at Al-Ardah and shelled farmland in the Quneitra countryside near the occupied Golan, the latest in near-daily incursions. The push came days after UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres, closing the first visit to Syria by a UN chief since 2009, called the violations of the 1974 line totally unacceptable.

July 27, 2026 · Economy

Damascus and Canada open talks on trade and investment as sanctions ease

Economy and Industry Minister Nidal al-Shaar met Canada's ambassador to Syria and Lebanon to discuss expanding trade, investment and industrial cooperation, following Ottawa's decision earlier this year to ease sanctions on Damascus. The meeting fits al-Sharaa's push to reconnect Syria to the global economy as the last major Western restrictions fall and the World Bank puts reconstruction needs near 216 billion dollars.

Iraq

July 27, 2026 · Energy

Iraq and Turkey reach a one-year deal to keep the Ceyhan pipeline pumping

Baghdad and Ankara have settled the terms of a one-year arrangement to keep crude flowing from northern Iraq to the Turkish port of Ceyhan past the July 27 expiry of the 1973 pipeline treaty, with formal signing expected within days. The deal preserves more than 200,000 barrels a day through the corridor, Iraq's main working export route while the Strait of Hormuz shutdown keeps southern shipments crippled, but leaves tariffs, Kurdish oil and Turkey's compensation demands unresolved.

The National
July 26, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Oman-mediated talks turn to managed Hormuz transit as Iraq eyes a Basra restart

With the US airstrike pause holding and Iran halting its retaliation, Oman-mediated talks now center on Iran running vessel transit through Hormuz under fewer restrictions, a framework that would let Iraq revive Basra flows that fell to a record low near 260,000 barrels a day in May. Brent has slipped back under 100 dollars as the war premium cools, though traders warn it will take months to clear the backlog even if the strait fully reopens.

Kuwait

July 27, 2026 · Defense

Kuwait ratifies a defense cooperation pact with Pakistan into law

Kuwait published a broad defense cooperation agreement with Pakistan in its official gazette, formalizing into law a pact first signed in 2023 as Gulf states harden their security posture through the Iran war. The accord covers military training, intelligence sharing, logistics, defense manufacturing and joint exercises, runs for five years with automatic renewal, and sets up a joint military committee to oversee it.

Kuwait Times
July 27, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Kuwait lines up for the OPEC+ August hike even as Hormuz caps its exports

Kuwait is set to join seven OPEC+ producers lifting output by a combined 188,000 barrels a day from August, yet the Hormuz shutdown still leaves it unable to move the extra barrels, with analysts warning actual output could fall as much as a quarter as storage fills. The squeeze underlines how the strait's closure has decoupled Kuwait's paper quota from what it can physically ship.

Kurdistan

July 27, 2026 · Energy High impact

Dana Gas restarts Khor Mor, lifting Kurdistan's blackout

Dana Gas has begun a gradual restart at the Khor Mor field after receiving security guarantees from the highest levels of the KRG and the federal government, aiming to reach full capacity near 750 million cubic feet a day by Tuesday. The restart returns about 2,500 megawatts to the grid and puts the Region back on a path to 24-hour electricity, reversing a shutdown that had stripped up to 80 percent of Kurdistan's power and cut Erbil's water by 30 percent.

July 27, 2026 · Security

Barzani says the drones that hit Erbil were launched from Mosul

Prime Minister Masrour Barzani said the drones that recently struck Erbil came from within Iraq, from the direction of Mosul, and pressed Baghdad to hold those responsible to account. He also blamed the Region's fuel shortage in part on Baghdad allocating Kurdistan less than its fair share for domestic use, a move he said lacks all justice, even as the Khor Mor restart begins to ease the power squeeze.

Baghdad transfers July salaries to the KRG as five months stay pending

The KRG finance ministry said Baghdad deposited about 956.9 billion dinars to cover July public sector salaries under the oil-for-salaries mechanism that ties the transfers to Erbil handing over 230,000 barrels a day of crude and 120 billion dinars a month in non-oil revenue. Officials note the federal government has now paid seven months of salaries this year with five still outstanding, keeping the broader revenue-sharing dispute alive.
