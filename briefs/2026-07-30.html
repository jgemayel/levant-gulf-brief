Syria

July 29, 2026 · Economy

Al-Sharaa opens the first Damascus International Fair since Assad's fall

President Ahmed al-Sharaa inaugurated the 62nd Damascus International Fair, the first edition since the collapse of the Assad government, with a Saudi delegation leading the opening ceremony and Riyadh and Damascus signing 11 agreements spanning energy, industry and investment. Officials framed the fair as a step to reconnect Syria to global markets and pull in Gulf and Western capital as sanctions ease.

Asharq Al-Awsat
July 29, 2026 · Energy

Saudi firms sign an agreement and six energy MoUs to rebuild Syria's power sector

Under the Saudi energy ministry's auspices, Saudi companies signed an agreement and six memoranda with Syria's Ministry of Energy, with ACWA Power set to study up to 1,000 megawatts of solar plus storage and 1,500 megawatts of wind. The package deepens a Gulf-funded reorientation of Syria's grid away from the old Russia and Iran axis and toward regional and Western partners.

Iraq

July 29, 2026 · Security High impact

Joint US and Saudi strikes hit Iran-backed militias across seven Iraqi provinces

US and Saudi aircraft struck Popular Mobilization Forces logistics and weapons sites in Baghdad, Wasit, Nineveh, Basra, Kirkuk, Karbala and Diyala early Wednesday, killing at least 20 fighters and at least four Iranian Revolutionary Guard advisers, in what CENTCOM cast as a response to more than 30 drone attacks on US troops and Saudi energy sites over 72 hours. The strikes marked the first time Riyadh joined the US directly on Iraqi soil, sharply widening the Iran war onto Iraqi territory and touching Kirkuk on the edge of the Kurdistan Region.

Al Jazeera
July 29, 2026 · Politics

Baghdad calls the strikes a "flagrant violation" and cancels al-Zaidi's Riyadh trip

The Ministerial Council for National Security, chaired by Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi, branded the raids on PMF bases a flagrant violation of Iraq's sovereignty and the sanctity of its land, and the premier's planned Thursday visit to Saudi Arabia was scrapped. Armed factions warned of a dangerous escalation while former prime minister Mohammed Shia al-Sudani cautioned against overstepping the principle of Islamic brotherhood, leaving Baghdad squeezed between its US and Gulf partners and the Iran-aligned bloc at home.

Al Jazeera
July 29, 2026 · Sanctions

US targets Iran's Hormuz "insurance" racket that has choked Iraqi exports

The State Department designated the IRGC-backed Persian Gulf Marine Insurance Company and Hormuz Safe Marine Services Authority for extorting fees from ships transiting the strait, alongside eight firms moving illicit Iranian crude to China and the UAE. The measures aim at the coercion regime that has thinned tanker traffic through Hormuz to a handful of ships a day and left Basra crude leaning almost entirely on the northern Ceyhan route.

Kuwait

July 29, 2026 · Diplomacy

Kuwait closes ranks with Riyadh as the strikes ripple across the Gulf

Foreign Minister Sheikh Jarrah Jaber Al-Sabah spoke by phone with Saudi counterpart Prince Faisal bin Farhan to coordinate on regional security and freedom of navigation, and Kuwait's foreign ministry condemned Iranian attacks on Saudi oil infrastructure in the Eastern Province as blatant aggression, reaffirming absolute solidarity with the kingdom. The alignment hardens as the joint operation next door in Iraq threatens fresh Iranian reprisals across the northern Gulf.

Gulf News
July 29, 2026 · Public Finance

Kuwait's 2025-26 deficit widens to 7.1 billion dinars, the worst since the pandemic

The closing account for fiscal year 2025-26 put the actual deficit at about 7.1 billion dinars, near 15 percent of GDP and the widest since the 2020-21 pandemic year, driven by weaker oil revenue as OPEC+ cuts unwound slowly and the Iran war disrupted exports. The result lands a day after Kuwait projected a still larger 9.8 billion dinar gap for 2026-27, underscoring how a producer with no pipeline around Hormuz absorbs the shock through its budget.

Kurdistan

July 29, 2026 · Security

Erbil's air defenses down a fresh wave of drones over Koya and Sherpazher

The Kurdistan Region Security Council said air defenses shot down five drones over Koya and its surroundings and one more near Sherpazher in a single 24-hour stretch, part of a run of interceptions over Erbil during the escalation. The barrage keeps threatening civilian, airport and energy sites and sits inside the wider Iran war now spilling openly across northern Iraq.

Kurdistan24
July 29, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Kurdistan's oil operators creep back online with Gulf Keystone leading

International producers are gradually restarting fields shut by the drone campaign, with Gulf Keystone resuming output at Shaikan after security assessments with the KRG, even as US operator HKN keeps Sarsang and Atrush offline. The attacks had knocked an estimated 140,000 to 150,000 barrels a day out of Kurdistan's production, and the phased return is tentative while the threat persists.

Iraqi News
July 29, 2026 · Energy

Baghdad pledges guarantees to shield Kurdistan's oil fields and foreign operators

The federal government moved to offer guarantees to protect Kurdistan's oil fields and the international companies operating them after drone and missile attacks forced repeated shutdowns. The pledge is meant to coax operators back and steady exports through Ceyhan, though Erbil wants concrete security on the ground before it counts on a durable restart.
