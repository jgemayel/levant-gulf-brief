Syria

July 2, 2026 · Security High impact

A bomb tears through a crowded central Damascus cafe, killing at least nine near the main courthouse

An improvised device weighing about one kilogram and packed with metal shrapnel detonated inside a busy cafe on al-Nasser Street in the al-Marjah district around 3:24pm on July 2, killing at least nine people and wounding 20, the Health Ministry said, with officials warning the toll could rise. The blast landed close to the Palace of Justice and drew no immediate claim of responsibility, and Damascus governor Maher Marwan said bad actors were trying to destabilise the country as interior forces reviewed camera footage and gathered forensic evidence.

Al Jazeera
July 1, 2026 · Justice

Syria opens public trials over the Sweida bloodshed that killed some 2,000 people last summer

A Damascus court began examining cases in public sessions from July 1 with defendants and their lawyers present, the government investigative committee said, over the July 2025 violence in the Druze-majority province that left more than 2,000 dead, including 789 Druze civilians by the Syrian Observatory's count. The committee has drawn its suspect list from the defence and interior ministries, Druze factions, and Bedouin and tribal fighters, framing the proceedings as an effort to hold anyone responsible to account regardless of side.

Iraq

July 2026 · Regional High impact

Khamenei's funeral rites reach Najaf and Karbala as Iran buries the leader killed in the war with Israel and the US

Seven days of funeral ceremonies for Ali Khamenei, killed aged 86 in a joint US and Israeli strike on February 28, begin July 4 and stretch across Tehran, Qom and Mashhad in Iran and the shrine cities of Najaf and Karbala in Iraq. An official reception attended by senior Iraqi officials led by Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi precedes the cortege's passage to the Imam Ali Shrine, after which the body travels by helicopter roughly 80 kilometers to the Shrine of Imam Hussein in Karbala before returning to Iran.

The National
July 2026 · Politics

Hosting the Khamenei cortege tests al-Zaidi's new government days before his Washington trip

Staging part of the funeral on Iraqi soil forces Baghdad to project Shiite solidarity with Tehran even as Prime Minister al-Zaidi presses a campaign to bring weapons under exclusive state control and prepares a mid-July White House visit built around energy investment and disarming factions outside the state. The optics of a state reception for Iran's slain leader, days before he meets Donald Trump, sharpen the balancing act between Iraq's Iran-aligned blocs and its push to reset ties with Washington.

Kuwait

July 2026 · Labor

Kuwait completes the digital transfer of 27,141 expat workers as the restricted-sector window closes

The Public Authority for Manpower said it automatically moved the residency permits of 27,141 expatriate workers out of restricted sectors into approved ones after the grace period under Ministerial Resolution No. 2 of 2026 closed, all handled digitally with original-employer consent and no visit to the authority required. Small and medium enterprises took the largest share at 16,048 approvals, followed by agriculture on 4,460, industry 3,300, livestock 3,087 and fisheries 246.

Arab Times
July 2026 · Energy

KPC offers July crude cargoes and drives output back toward 2 million barrels a day as Hormuz shipping reopens

Kuwait Petroleum Corporation is marketing Kuwait Export Crude for July delivery in cargoes of 2 million barrels each, priced against Oman and Dubai on a delivered basis, as it rebuilds shipments halted when the Strait of Hormuz crisis shut its only export route. Output that collapsed to about 573,000 barrels a day in May, the steepest fall of any Gulf producer given the lack of a pipeline bypass, is being pushed back toward 2 million and eventually the prewar level near 2.6 million.

Kurdistan

July 3, 2026 · Diplomacy

Nechirvan Barzani travels to Tehran for Khamenei's funeral and meets Araghchi and Pezeshkian

Kurdistan Region President Nechirvan Barzani arrived in Tehran on July 3 to attend the burial rites of Iran's late supreme leader, holding talks with Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi and being received by President Masoud Pezeshkian. The sides discussed trade, economic cooperation and border connections between Iran, Iraq and the Region, and reviewed the aftermath of the war between Iran, the United States and Israel that killed Khamenei.

Iraqi News
July 2026 · Politics

The KDP signals a new phase of dialogue with the PUK to break the 20-month cabinet deadlock

Kurdistan endowment and religious affairs minister Pishtiwan Sadiq said the KDP is readying a new phase of political dialogue with the PUK to finally seat the Region's tenth cabinet, more than 20 months after the October 2024 vote, with a KDP delegation visiting PUK headquarters in Sulaimani to stress unity and stability. The signal marks movement after the legislature slipped into a two-month summer recess with government formation frozen between the KDP's 39 seats and the PUK's 23.
