Syria

July 31, 2026 · Economy AR

Syria's Assad-era pound loses legal tender as the currency overhaul closes

Old Assad-era banknotes ceased to be legal tender on July 31, ending a redesign that the central bank says has swapped out roughly 80 percent of the currency since January, with a five-year window now open for holders to redeem old notes in batches of at least 100 through the bank's Damascus headquarters. The cutoff followed weeks of market confusion and daily disputes as merchants refused the old pound ahead of the deadline. Translated from Enab Baladi.

July 31, 2026 · Security

Syrian forces roll up two ISIS cells in Aleppo and al-Bab

Internal security and general intelligence forces arrested seven suspected Daesh members in raids on hideouts in Aleppo city and the town of al-Bab, seizing explosives, improvised devices and a cache of weapons the interior ministry said were staged for attacks on government facilities. The operation is part of a widening state campaign to dismantle ISIS networks as the group probes for openings in the post-Assad security vacuum.

Iraq

July 31, 2026 · Security High impact

Iran's Guard says it struck two US-escorted tankers in the Strait of Hormuz

The Revolutionary Guard said on July 31 it hit two oil tankers attempting to cross Hormuz under US military escort and forced four others to turn back, insisting the strait stays closed to any vessel that does not coordinate with its navy. US and British maritime monitors had not confirmed the strikes, but the claim kept Brent near 90 dollars and left Iraq's export artery, which normally carries the bulk of the country's crude and roughly 90 percent of its budget revenue, effectively bottled up.

The Washington Times
July 31, 2026 · Energy

Baghdad presses Iran for tanker passage as southern exports slow to a trickle

With Basra crude forced down to about a quarter of normal flows, Iraq is still pressing Tehran to wave chartered tankers safely through Hormuz, a request Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi carried to Iran on his July 23 visit and that Iranian officials have neither accepted nor refused. Kpler tracking put strait transits as low as a single tanker on some late-July days, leaving the northern Ceyhan line as Iraq's only reliable outlet.

Kuwait

July 31, 2026 · Public Finance

Kuwait ramps borrowing and freezes spending as Hormuz chokes its oil income

The government has told ministries to rein in spending and leaned harder on debt markets, issuing about 27 billion dollars since October, close to a quarter of the 30 billion dinar ceiling set under the new 50-year debt law, as the shut strait keeps crude revenue offline. The scramble follows a 2025-26 deficit that ballooned nearly sevenfold to 7.1 billion dinars, the widest since the pandemic year.

July 31, 2026 · Energy

Kuwaiti crude climbs to 86.69 dollars as the tanker strikes revive the war premium

Kuwait Petroleum Corporation put the official price of Kuwaiti crude at 86.69 dollars a barrel on Thursday, up 1.69 dollars in a single session as fresh Hormuz strikes rebuilt the risk premium. The gain is cold comfort for a producer whose barrels remain trapped behind the strait, so higher prices do little while exports cannot sail.

Kurdistan

July 31, 2026 · Fiscal

Baghdad transfers July public-sector salaries to the KRG

The KRG finance ministry said Baghdad deposited about 956.9 billion dinars to cover July salaries for Kurdistan Region public employees, keeping the salaries-for-oil arrangement between Erbil and Baghdad ticking over. The transfer lands with several months still owed under a deal that has trailed the region's long-running fight over its share of federal entitlements.

A fresh drone wave hammers Iranian-Kurdish opposition bases near Erbil

Opposition commanders counted at least 14 drone strikes since Monday on camps belonging to the Komala party, the Democratic Party of Iranian Kurdistan and a PUK-linked facility around Doli Alana, Soran and Khalifan, with the US-led coalition intercepting drones over Erbil and Soran. The barrage extends a campaign that, by mid-July, had thrown more than 900 drones and missiles at the region since late February, killing 29 people and wounding scores.
