Syria

June 27, 2026 · Regional

Syria condemns Iranian drone attacks on Bahrain as the US-Iran war reignites and again threatens Hormuz

As the war flared again before dawn on June 27, with American airstrikes on Iranian missile, drone and radar sites and Iranian drones striking Bahrain, Syria's Foreign Ministry called the attack on Bahrain a flagrant violation of the kingdom's sovereignty and voiced full support for its security. The statement lines post-Assad Damascus up squarely with the Gulf bloc against Tehran as the fighting again threatens the recently reopened Strait of Hormuz.

SANA
June 27, 2026 · Security

Israeli forces push an armored incursion into Jamla in western Daraa, searching homes near the Golan line

A column of eight Israeli military vehicles and a tank moved from the Tal Abu al-Ghaithar checkpoint through Wadi al-Raqqad into Jamla in the western Daraa countryside on June 27, searching several homes as drones circled overhead, with a separate raid reported in southern Quneitra. The operation extends near-daily Israeli violations of the 1974 disengagement line across Syria's south.

SANA
June 27, 2026 · Economy

Damascus cuts fuel prices by up to 20 percent to ease cost-of-living strain

Energy Minister Mohammad al-Bashir approved reductions of roughly 14 to 20 percent across petroleum products on June 27, lowering 90-octane gasoline about 20 percent to 125 pounds a liter, diesel about 14 percent to 107 pounds, and a household gas cylinder to 1,500 pounds, citing a review of market indicators. It is one of the first notable downward fuel moves since the currency redenomination took hold.

Iraq

June 27, 2026 · Regional High impact

Baghdad braces to host Khamenei's funeral procession through Najaf and Karbala on July 8

Iran says the body of Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, assassinated in an Israeli strike on February 28, will be flown to Najaf on July 8 for ceremonies at the Imam Ali shrine and a mass procession in Karbala before burial in Mashhad on July 9, though Iraqi and Iranian officials caution that final arrangements are still being set. Routing the funeral through Iraq's two holiest Shia cities hands Baghdad an enormous crowd-control and security task days before Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's planned Washington trip.

Iran's Araghchi arrives in Baghdad to brief Iraq on the US talks and coordinate the Khamenei procession

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi arrived in Baghdad on June 28 to brief Iraqi leaders on the Switzerland talks with Washington and to coordinate Khamenei's funeral route through Iraq, putting al-Zaidi's mediator role on display as the war reignites. The visit lands a day after Iran struck US sites in Kuwait and Bahrain and a ship in the Strait of Hormuz.

Shafaq News
June 27, 2026 · Governance

Al-Zaidi says detained oil official offered a 200 million dollar bribe as graft seizures top 86 million

Al-Zaidi told local media on June 27 that detained Oil Ministry undersecretary Adnan al-Jumaili offered a 200 million dollar bribe through a mediator to get his case dropped, as the Supreme Judicial Council put seizures in the probe at about 86 million dollars plus 70 properties, 21 vehicles and roughly 3 kilograms of gold. Former Salah al-Din governor Raed al-Jubouri was arrested on June 24 after al-Jumaili's confessions implicated him, in the biggest corruption case of the new government.

Kuwait

June 28, 2026 · Security High impact

Iran strikes US sites in Kuwait again as air defenses intercept missiles over Ali al-Salem base

Iran's Revolutionary Guard said early on June 28 it had launched missiles and drones at US military sites in Kuwait and Bahrain in retaliation for American strikes inside Iran, and Kuwait's Defense Ministry said its air defenses intercepted incoming fire over the Ali al-Salem Air Base. The spokesman condemned what he called criminal Iranian aggression, the second time in a month that the war has reached Kuwaiti soil.

NPR
June 27, 2026 · Energy

Renewed Hormuz attacks shadow Kuwait's fragile oil-export restart

A ship struck in the Strait of Hormuz on June 27 prompted the International Maritime Organization to pause its evacuation of stranded vessels, with Brent swinging back above 76 dollars before easing to about 74. Kuwaiti crude exports, near 1.2 million barrels a day before the war, had collapsed toward zero in April and were only just restarting through ship-to-ship transfers off Oman when the fighting flared again, clouding KPC's recently issued July delivery tender.

Kurdistan

June 27, 2026 · Security

Explosive drone hits an Iranian Kurdish opposition camp north of Erbil, with no casualties reported

An explosive drone struck a camp belonging to an Iranian Kurdish opposition group north of Erbil on June 27, causing no casualties because the site had recently been evacuated, security sources told Reuters. The strike fits a wartime pattern of Iranian and allied-militia attacks on Kurdish opposition sites inside the Kurdistan Region as the US-Iran war reignites.

June 26, 2026 · Energy

Norway's DNO restarts the Tawke and Peshkabir fields as the KRG oil sector recovers field by field

The KRG said Norway's DNO resumed production at its Tawke and Peshkabir fields late in the week, the latest step in a field-by-field restart after operators halted on February 28 when the air campaign began. DNO had averaged about 39,600 barrels a day in the region before the war and is relaunching a previously announced eight-well drilling campaign across the two fields.

June 27, 2026 · Energy

Baghdad presses Kirkuk-Ceyhan exports toward 770,000 barrels a day to offset Hormuz losses

Baghdad is pushing to lift flows through the Kirkuk-Ceyhan pipeline toward 770,000 barrels a day, including about 400,000 from Kurdistan fields, to claw back revenue lost while the Strait of Hormuz stays contested. National output had dropped from roughly 4.3 million barrels a day to between 1.3 and 1.4 million as southern shipping seized up, leaving the northern route through Turkey as Iraq's main outlet and raising the stakes on the Erbil-Baghdad export deal.

KDP-PUK rift over the next KRG cabinet flares again as Barzani relaunches party talks

The feud between the KDP and PUK over the tenth KRG cabinet flared again, with the KDP accusing the PUK of a policy of imposition nearly two years after the October 2024 vote, as the interior ministry and the PUK's insistence on negotiating jointly with the New Generation Movement remain the main sticking points. On June 27 the KDP said its political bureau, at President Nechirvan Barzani's direction, had begun fresh meetings with the region's parties to try to break the deadlock.
