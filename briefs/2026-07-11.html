Syria

July 10, 2026 · Security AR

Syria says it has dismantled the ISIS cell behind the Damascus bombings that struck during Macron's visit

Interior Minister Anas Khattab announced the arrest of the entire network responsible for the July 7 bombings in central Damascus, which killed one person and wounded 36, with security forces and the General Intelligence Directorate running simultaneous raids in al-Qutayfah, Sayyida Zeinab, the Qudsaya suburb and Ash al-Warwar. Investigators said surveillance footage identified one suspect first before the rest of the cell, described as affiliated with the Islamic State, was tracked down. Translated from SANA.

SANA
July 8, 2026 · Diplomacy High impact

Washington moves to lift Syria's 47-year terrorism designation, effective in 45 days

The State Department notified Congress on July 8 that the administration intends to rescind Syria's designation as a State Sponsor of Terrorism, a status in force since 1979, following a meeting between President Trump and Ahmad al-Sharaa on the sidelines of the NATO summit in Ankara. The rescission takes effect in 45 days unless lawmakers block it, and once complete it would leave only Iran, North Korea and Cuba on the US blacklist, clearing a major obstacle to reconstruction finance and normalised trade.

Al Jazeera
July 9, 2026 · Security

Damascus tightens security and shutters its airspace as the region tips back toward war

Syria closed its national airspace and stepped up checkpoints around the capital in the days after the Damascus bombings, moving in parallel with a renewed round of US and Iranian strikes across the region that has pushed most neighbouring air corridors back into high-risk status. The transitional government is trying to hold a fragile security line at home while avoiding entanglement in the wider Iran confrontation, a balancing act sharpened by the return of open fighting to the Gulf.

Iraq

July 9, 2026 · Security High impact

The 2026 Iran war reignites and Iraq is again caught in the crossfire

The ceasefire that ended the spring war collapsed after President Trump declared it over in response to Iranian attacks on commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz, and US forces resumed strikes on Iran on July 7 while Tehran and its Iraqi proxies hit back across the region on July 9. Drones were launched at the Ain al-Asad airbase in Anbar, air defences were activated near US positions, and Iraq restricted its airspace as the Baghdad flight region reverted to high-risk, leaving Baghdad to absorb strikes it did not order.

Iraqi News
July 9, 2026 · Politics

Al-Zaidi presses his anti-graft and disarmament drive and stakes out neutrality days before Washington

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi said there are no red lines in protecting public funds and vowed to press ahead with the anti-corruption campaign that has already netted dozens of officials, insisting weapons will come under state control once the September 30 deadline for armed factions passes. With the US-Iran fight flaring again, he stressed Iraqi neutrality and cast Baghdad as a channel for de-escalation, a stance he carries into his July 15 talks in Washington.

Kuwait

July 9, 2026 · Security High impact

Kuwait intercepts three ballistic missiles, a cruise missile and ten drones over its airspace

The Defence Ministry said Kuwaiti air defences detected and downed three ballistic missiles, one cruise missile and ten hostile drones over the country early Thursday, with Iran saying it had targeted a Patriot interceptor system on Kuwaiti soil in retaliation for US strikes. Falling debris caused material damage in several areas, injured one person and knocked out overhead power transmission lines, and the interception put Kuwait squarely inside the widening arc of the renewed conflict alongside Qatar, Bahrain and Jordan.

Zawya
July 9, 2026 · Economy

Kuwaiti crude climbs above 76 dollars as the war revives Hormuz risk

Kuwait Export Crude jumped about 4.36 dollars to 76.03 dollars a barrel as the renewed fighting lifted Brent toward 79 dollars and WTI toward 75, up from roughly 72 and 69 at the start of the week, with the reversal of the ceasefire's sanctions relief on Iranian oil adding to the squeeze. Prices remain well below the near 120 dollar peak of the spring war, and the path from here hinges on whether cargoes keep moving through the Strait of Hormuz.

Kurdistan

July 9, 2026 · Security

Iran's renewed strikes hit Kurdish opposition camps near Erbil, reviving the spring bombardment

As the war reignited, Iranian missiles and drones again struck camps of Iranian-Kurdish opposition groups in the Erbil area, with the Revolutionary Guard describing the salvo as a punitive response and Kurdish factions bracing for a wider campaign. The strikes revive memories of the spring bombardment that the KRG says brought more than 800 drone and missile attacks across the Region between late February and April, killing 20 people and wounding more than 120.

July 9, 2026 · Aviation

Erbil flights are cut again as Iraqi airspace tightens over the Region

Several flights to Erbil International Airport were cancelled as US and Iranian fire exchanges resumed and Iraqi authorities restricted the airspace over the Region, a fresh blow to a hub whose 38-day spring shutdown cost Kurdistan's airports an estimated 400 million dollars. The disruption lands just as the KRG was trying to steady its economy on the back of the revived oil export deal and a phased return of international operators.
