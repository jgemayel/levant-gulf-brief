May 18, 2026 · Trade and customs AR

President al-Sharaa issues Decrees 109 and 110 of 2026, the first full rewrite of Syria's customs law since 2006 in 264 articles, hardening the Israel boycott and resetting the import-export perimeter

President al-Sharaa issued Decrees 109 and 110 of 2026 on Monday introducing a new General Customs Law of 264 articles to replace the 2006 framework, with Article 112 codifying the entry ban on goods that breach boycott laws, including the Israel boycott, alongside the standing prohibition on Israeli citizens entering Syria. The rewrite lands after Decree 117 of 2026 waived customs-violation fines on pre-December 2024 cases earlier in May and after a presidential reshuffle of the import-export committee, positioning the Damascus customs and border regime for a post-sanctions reopening while signalling domestically that the Israel track stays on the formal hard line.

May 19, 2026 · Power and water

KRG dams reach full capacity with over 9 billion cubic metres in storage across 25 dams and 178 ponds, Dukan within 1.15 metres of overflow after record Erbil and Sulaimani rainfall

KRG dams and water reservoirs director-general Abdulrahman Khani said Tuesday that water storage in the Region has crossed 9 billion cubic metres after almost all 25 dams and 178 artificial ponds reached full capacity, with only Sulaimani's Dukan, now 1.15 metres from overflow, and Erbil's Gomaspan still filling. The infrastructure base now sits on rainfall readings of 719 mm in Erbil city, 1,054 mm in Sulaimani, 921 mm in Duhok, 1,109 mm in Halabja and 2,140 mm in Mergasor through late April, with Darbandikhan holding around 3 billion cubic metres and Duhok over 50 million cubic metres on its own.
