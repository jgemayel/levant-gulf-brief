Syria

July 5, 2026 · Politics High impact

Syria's first post-Assad parliament is set to convene July 6 as al-Sharaa completes the 210-seat assembly

Interim President Ahmad al-Sharaa has finalised the 210-member People's Assembly, directly appointing 70 legislators, 55 men and 15 women, alongside the 140 members selected through indirect electoral colleges over the past eight months, lifting the number of women in the chamber to 22. The body holds its inaugural session on Monday July 6, when members are sworn in and a presidential council is elected, opening the first legislature since the fall of Bashar al-Assad in December 2024.

Israel deepens its footprint in occupied southern Syria as a settler-backed entrenchment project takes shape

Israeli forces are consolidating their hold on the buffer zone seized after Assad's fall, with reporting describing a settler movement pressing a "Greater Israel" agenda into the south and a military-linked agricultural project meant to normalise a permanent presence in the area under Israeli control. Iraq

July 5, 2026 · Politics High impact

Iraq moves to fill the defence and interior posts and complete al-Zaidi's cabinet before the Washington summit

Parliament is moving in a July 5 session to fill the nine portfolios left vacant when Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi won a partial confidence vote on May 14, among them defence, interior, planning, higher education, culture, labour, youth and sport, migration, and reconstruction. Seating loyal security ministers is central to al-Zaidi's drive to bring weapons under exclusive state control, and completing the government anchors his standing days before a mid-July White House meeting with Donald Trump built around energy investment and disarmament.

Al-Zaidi's disarmament order splits the Shiite factions ahead of the September 30 coalition exit

Baghdad has ordered pro-Iran armed groups to surrender their weapons to the state by September 30, the date set for the US-led anti-ISIS coalition to wind down, and the demand is fracturing the Coordination Framework. Asaib Ahl al-Haq and Kataeb Imam Ali have signalled they will hand over brigades, personnel and arms, while Kataib Hezbollah, Kataib Sayyid al-Shuhada and Harakat al-Nujaba have publicly rejected disarmament and pledged to keep their arsenals for "resistance."

Kuwait

July 2026 · Energy

Kuwait overhauls its oil-sector governance as a new KPC law takes effect

A decree-law amending the 1980 statute that established Kuwait Petroleum Corporation has entered into force as Law No. 67 of 2026, modernising the framework governing the state oil holding and reaffirming the Supreme Petroleum Council, chaired by Prime Minister Sheikh Ahmad Al-Abdullah Al-Sabah, as the sector's top authority. The amendment is one of roughly 140 decrees issued since the government began ruling without an elected assembly, part of a reform drive aiming to modernise about 250 laws, a quarter of Kuwait's legislation, by the end of 2026.

Kuwait Times
July 2026 · Energy

Kuwait rebuilds crude supply on the OPEC+ output track after its wartime collapse

Kuwait is restoring output alongside a July increase agreed by eight OPEC+ producers, which lifted the group's targets by 411,000 barrels a day, after its production cratered to roughly 578,000 barrels a day in May when the Strait of Hormuz shut and left the country without a pipeline bypass. With force majeure lifted and repairs finished ahead of schedule, KPC is guiding supply back toward the prewar level near 2.628 million barrels a day, having marketed July export cargoes as the strait reopened.

Kurdistan

July 2026 · Fiscal

The KRG presses Baghdad for July and August wages under a draft SOMO oil-for-salaries framework

Erbil is urging Baghdad to release overdue July and August salaries without delay as the two sides negotiate a draft framework tying resumed exports to wage payments, under which all export-bound crude, about 230,000 to 240,000 barrels a day, is handed to Iraq's marketer SOMO while the Region keeps some 50,000 barrels for domestic use. The KRG says Baghdad has delivered only 41 percent of its entitlements over three years, 24.3 trillion of 58.3 trillion dinars, and warns the shortfall is squeezing public employees.

KDP and PUK ready talks on the federal presidency alongside the stalled KRG cabinet

Delegations from the two ruling Kurdish parties are preparing to meet on both the Region's long-delayed tenth cabinet and the shape of a unified Kurdish position in Baghdad, including the federal presidency, as the KDP opens a fresh phase of dialogue with the PUK. The federal track dovetails with Baghdad's own push to complete al-Zaidi's government, giving Erbil's internal deadlock a direct bearing on the balance being struck in the capital.
