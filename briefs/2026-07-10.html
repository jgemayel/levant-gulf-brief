Syria

July 8, 2026 · Diplomacy

Washington moves to strike Syria from its terrorism blacklist, opening the door to trade and reconstruction finance

The State Department began the process on July 8 to rescind Syria's nearly five-decade designation as a State Sponsor of Terrorism, sending Congress the required notice ahead of a 45-day review, days after President Trump met Ahmad al-Sharaa on the sidelines of a NATO summit in Ankara. Removing the label would clear one of the last major legal barriers to international banking, trade and reconstruction financing for Damascus as it courts Gulf and European capital.

US Department of State
July 9, 2026 · Security

Syria captures the cell behind the Damascus bombings and points to an Islamic State link

Interior Minister Anas Khattab said on July 9 that internal security and intelligence forces had arrested every member of the cell behind the July 7 twin blasts that wounded 18 during Macron's visit, following simultaneous raids across Damascus and its countryside. A Syrian security source cited by Israeli broadcaster KAN tied the cell to Islamic State, though the group has claimed neither those attacks nor the July 2 courthouse cafe bombing that killed 10.

Iraq

July 9, 2026 · Security High impact

The US-Iran ceasefire collapses and fresh strikes leave Iraq exposed on the front line again

The truce that had paused the spring war unravelled this week as US forces bombed more than 80 targets across Iran and Tehran answered with drones and missiles aimed at Gulf states and US bases, with Trump declaring the ceasefire "over" on July 8 while insisting he did not expect a return to full-scale war. Iraq sits in the middle once more, its airspace repeatedly closed and Iran-aligned militias resuming attacks on US positions near Erbil and Baghdad, sharpening the pressure on al-Zaidi's September 30 deadline for armed factions to disarm.

Al Jazeera
July 8, 2026 · Energy

Oil jumps and Hormuz traffic thins as the renewed war threatens the Strait

Brent settled 5.2 percent higher at 78.02 dollars and WTI rose 4.4 percent to 73.52 dollars on July 8 after the US strikes and Trump's threat to reimpose a naval blockade, unwinding a slide that had taken crude back toward pre-war levels. Traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, the outlet for the bulk of Iraqi and Kuwaiti crude, fell to about 13 vessels in 24 hours against roughly 110 a day before the war, a squeeze that lands hardest on the region's largest OPEC producer.

Kuwait

July 9, 2026 · Security High impact

Kuwait's air defenses down Iranian missiles and drones for a second straight day

Kuwaiti air defenses intercepted three ballistic missiles, a cruise missile and 10 drones over the country early on July 9, a day after downing two ballistic missiles and 13 drones, as Iran retaliated for US strikes by targeting a Patriot battery on Kuwaiti soil. Falling debris damaged several sites, knocked overhead power lines out of service and injured one person, though the Defense Ministry reported no direct hit.

Zawya
July 2026 · Energy

Renewed Gulf fighting puts Kuwait's oil-export recovery back at risk

KPC had only just lifted its wartime force majeure on June 19 and offered July cargoes of 2 million barrels each while pushing output back toward 2 million barrels a day from a May low near 573,000, a rebound now threatened as attacks resume and Hormuz shipping thins. A fresh force majeure would again strand the crude that funds the bulk of the state budget.

Asharq Al-Awsat
July 2026 · Economy

Kuwait's budget deficit widens 13 percent as oil income falls short

The Finance Ministry reported the annual deficit widened about 13 percent to 7.1 billion dinars, near 23 billion dollars, with total revenue running roughly 10 percent below forecast and oil receipts 11 percent short after the wartime export halt. New levies on multinationals and a tax on harmful goods are projected to raise nearly 1.5 billion dollars in the 2027-28 fiscal year to help close the gap.

Kurdistan

July 9, 2026 · Security

War's return puts the Kurdistan Region back on alert as a drone strike hits an Erbil camp

A drone strike hit camps of the Iranian Kurdish opposition group PJAK near Erbil on July 9 as the reignited war spread back into northern Iraq, the latest of some 200 missile and drone attacks on the Region since late February blamed on Iran and Iran-aligned militias. US bases and consular sites around Erbil, hit repeatedly through the spring, are again on heightened guard.

July 8, 2026 · Fiscal

The Baghdad-Erbil oil and salary deal stalls again, leaving 1.2 million workers unpaid

The federal Council of Ministers left the Kurdish oil-and-salary file off its July 8 agenda, dashing July 7 reports that a deal to authorize KRG exports through Ceyhan had reached its final stages even after a Kurdish technical delegation spent days in Baghdad. More than 1.2 million public employees in the Region have gone unpaid since May, with the sticking points a federal demand for full control of oil revenue against Erbil's insistence on keeping a share of crude plus a 2 to 3 dollar transport fee on top of the 16 dollar production cost.
