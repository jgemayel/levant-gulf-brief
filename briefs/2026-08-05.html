Syria

August 4, 2026 · Diplomacy

Al-Sharaa meets SDF commander Abdi as the integration deal snags on the army question

Syrian President Ahmad al-Sharaa met Syrian Democratic Forces commander Mazloum Abdi to push implementation of the January 29 US-brokered accord that halted fighting and commits the Kurdish-led force to fold into the state. The talks snagged on the same core sticking point, with Damascus wanting SDF units diluted across the new army while Abdi holds out for his commanders to enter as formed blocs and for a Kurdish-majority police in the northeast, even as a presidential aide said the integration has reached its final stages.

Israeli forces stage fresh raids into Quneitra and Daraa in southern Syria

Israeli forces pushed six military vehicles to the quarries junction near Jubata al-Khashab in northern Quneitra on Tuesday, setting up a temporary checkpoint and searching passersby before withdrawing, after another unit entered Maariya in the western Daraa countryside and searched homes with no reported arrests. Iraq

August 4, 2026 · Energy High impact

A deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz moves within reach as Iran and Oman near a channel accord

A deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz moved within reach as Iran and Oman closed on an arrangement to split traffic into an Iran-controlled inbound channel and an Omani-side outbound lane, funded by a service fee shared equally between the two, with Qatar circulating an interim proposal and US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent saying an agreement could land within a day. Brent slid below 80 dollars and US crude fell toward 75, down more than 10 percent on the week, while fewer than 10 ships a day still transit the waterway that carries the bulk of Iraq's crude. PM al-Zaidi lands in Washington chasing US energy deals and a route around Hormuz

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi flew to Washington for his first foreign trip since taking office, seeking oil and gas agreements with US firms and a meeting with President Trump to anchor what he called a new strategic partnership. Al-Zaidi is pitching a plan to lift Iraqi output toward 7 million barrels per day within three years and an Energy and Development Fund seeded by revenue from 500,000 barrels per day, part of a drive to cut the country's reliance on the Strait of Hormuz.

Kuwait

August 4, 2026 · Energy

Oil's slide toward 75 dollars eases the war premium but widens Kuwait's budget gap

The drop in oil toward 75 dollars on hopes of a Hormuz reopening cut both ways for Kuwait, easing the war premium while dragging prices further below the roughly 90.5 dollar level the country needs to balance a budget already facing a deficit penciled near 9.8 billion dinars. Kuwait Petroleum has signaled that even once the strait reopens a full return to output will take 10 to 12 weeks, with about 70 percent of normal production back within six to eight, after the blockade drove April crude exports to zero for the first time since 1991.

Kurdistan

August 3, 2026 · Security High impact

A coalition drawdown pulls Western air defenses out of Erbil as drone attacks intensify

US, British, French and German forces pulled hundreds of troops and their air defenses out of Erbil in a 72-hour drawdown, moving the remaining US Patriot batteries to Jordan and a British Rapid Sentry system to Cyprus and stripping the surveillance balloons over the city, without notifying the Kurdistan Regional Government. The withdrawal, cast by officials as a step toward a September 30 exit deadline, left Erbil badly exposed as Iran-aligned militias kept up drone salvoes, with the coalition downing eight explosive-laden drones over the city inside one half-hour window.

August 4, 2026 · Energy

Pearl Petroleum lays out a 10.2 billion dollar plan to double Kurdistan gas output

Pearl Petroleum laid out a 10.2 billion dollar investment plan running to 2035 to more than double natural gas output from the Khor Mor and Chemchemal fields in Sulaymaniyah, lifting production from 750 million cubic feet per day toward 1.65 billion. The consortium led by Dana Gas and Crescent Petroleum, which has sunk more than 3.9 billion dollars into the fields since 2007, framed the roadmap as building on the 1.1 billion dollar KM250 plant it commissioned last October.
