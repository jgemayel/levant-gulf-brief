Syria

June 20, 2026 · Security

Islamic State kills two Syrian soldiers near Manbij and claims a new wave of operations against the al-Sharaa government in the northeastern Aleppo corridor

Islamic State claimed an attack in northeastern Aleppo province near the city of Manbij on Saturday that killed two Syrian Defence Ministry soldiers, in an incident Syrian state media initially attributed to unknown assailants before the IS Telegram claim landed. The attack is the latest in a series IS has run against al-Sharaa's forces since announcing a new operational phase against the transitional government in February, with IS describing the Manbij belt as a priority corridor for the current cycle.

Jerusalem Post
June 19, 2026 · Security

Israeli Defence Minister Katz says the IDF will not withdraw from Syria's security zones and warns al-Sharaa against interference, speaking from inside the buffer position

Defence Minister Israel Katz visited the IDF position inside Syria's buffer zone on Friday and stated that Israeli forces will remain deployed in the security zones across Syria, Lebanon, and Gaza without a defined timeline, framing the presence as essential protection for Israeli communities against jihadist organizations. Katz directed an implicit message at President al-Sharaa, saying he should "stay in Syria and not interfere in our affairs" and that interference would prompt an Israeli response, in what amounts to the clearest public articulation yet of Israel's conditions for coexistence along the buffer-zone perimeter.

June 20, 2026 · Diplomacy

Vice President Vance flies to Switzerland for Iran nuclear and Lebanon ceasefire talks as Israeli strikes on Nabatieh kill 16 people, keeping southern Lebanon under active fire

Vice President JD Vance departed for Geneva on Saturday alongside special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, citing Lebanon ceasefire progress and the nuclear file as the two primary negotiating goals, with Qatar and Pakistan also in the mediation channel. Israeli strikes on the Nabatieh district in south Lebanon killed 16 civilians on the same day as the announcement, raising fresh questions about the ceasefire's durability and keeping the Lebanon-Syria border security corridor under active military pressure for Damascus during the talks.

Iraq

June 19, 2026 · Finance High impact

Iraq formally commits to closing FATF anti-money-laundering deficiencies as PM al-Zaidi reshuffles the Central Bank governor and Finance Ministry undersecretary ahead of the July Washington visit

The US Embassy in Baghdad announced on Friday that Iraq officially agreed to address long-standing strategic deficiencies in its AML and counter-terrorism-financing systems under FATF terms, with Washington warning that failure to meet institutional milestones would risk full financial blacklisting. The commitment coincides with PM al-Zaidi's first broad administrative overhaul, which has already replaced the Central Bank of Iraq governor and the Finance Ministry undersecretary; reporting from multiple Iraqi outlets indicates more than 100 officials across government agencies are in scope as al-Zaidi prepares the institutional landscape before his mid-July White House meeting with President Trump.

June 20, 2026 · Security

Iraq signals an imminent overhaul of military and security leadership as al-Zaidi moves to align command structures with the state-arms-monopoly agenda

Iraqi officials indicated a broad reshuffle of military and security leadership is imminent, part of al-Zaidi's program to align the command architecture with the state-monopoly-on-arms agenda before his July Washington trip. Kuwait

June 21, 2026 · Aviation

Qatar Airways resumes daily Doha-Kuwait City service from Terminal 4 as Gulf carriers push aviation recovery toward 90 percent of pre-disruption volumes

Qatar Airways restarted its daily Doha-Kuwait City passenger route on Sunday, operating through Terminal 4 of Kuwait International Airport after the June 3 Iranian drone and missile strike that killed one person and wounded 63 at the facility and forced a full suspension of flights. Gulf carriers have now collectively recovered to about 90 percent of pre-disruption volumes, with Terminal 1 remaining closed pending structural assessment. Oman Air is next in the recovery sequence, scheduled to resume Kuwait service from Terminal 4 on June 25.

June 20, 2026 · Justice

Justice Minister Al-Sumait reports a 33 percent fall in domestic violence cases at the Public Prosecution in the first three months under the new protection law

Cases registered with the Public Prosecution under the Domestic Violence Protection Law fell from 486 in the comparable prior-year period to 328 in the law's first three months, a 33 percent decline, Justice Minister Nasser Al-Sumait said on Saturday. Al-Sumait attributed the drop to improved complaint-handling procedures and a built-in reconciliation pathway that routes family-dispute cases differently from genuine protection referrals, framing the data as evidence the law is reshaping how domestic disputes enter the justice system rather than simply suppressing reporting.

June 19, 2026 · Politics

Kuwait marks 65 years since the June 19, 1961 independence declaration with flag-raising ceremonies across all six governorates

Kuwait observed the 65th anniversary of the day in 1961 when the late Amir Sheikh Abdullah Al-Salem Al-Sabah formally ended the British protectorate agreement and declared independence. Official ceremonies and flag-raising events were held across all six governorates on Friday; the government framed the anniversary as a milestone in Kuwait's modern state-building journey and a marker of the country's evolution from protectorate status into a sovereign actor with active Gulf and global diplomatic reach.

Kurdistan

June 16, 2026 · Government

PM Masrour Barzani presses US envoy Tom Barrack for swift KRG parliamentary reactivation, framing it as the binding fix for the 19-month cabinet deadlock

KRG Prime Minister Masrour Barzani received Tom Barrack in Erbil on Tuesday for discussions covering KRG energy investment, KRG-Baghdad outstanding issues, and the regional security picture, with Barzani pressing Barrack specifically on parliamentary reactivation as "absolutely paramount" to breaking the stalemate that has blocked the tenth KRG cabinet since the October 2024 elections. The meeting is part of Barrack's first joint Iraq-Syria envoy sweep and follows his June 15 session with PM al-Zaidi in Baghdad, where energy cooperation including Chevron's West Qurna-2 and Nasiriyah mandate and the HKN, Western Zagros, and Hunt resumption guarantees were also confirmed.

June 17, 2026 · Fiscal

KRG caretaker cabinet pushes Baghdad for the 120-billion-IQD monthly federal allocation, orders faster pension payments, and directs the Natural Resources Ministry to maximize oil export throughput

The KRG Council of Ministers, chaired by PM Masrour Barzani and attended by Deputy PM Qubad Talabani, met Wednesday and issued three priority directives: formally requesting that Baghdad treat the 120-billion-IQD monthly transfer as a structural entitlement rather than a discretionary allocation; ordering accelerated pension processing for retired employees who have not yet started receiving salaries; and instructing the Natural Resources Ministry and the KRG oil negotiating delegation to push Kurdistan-pipeline exports to maximum capacity with all revenues remitted to the federal treasury. KRG output currently stands at around 350,000 barrels a day, below the pre-closure peak and leaving substantial headroom the caretaker cabinet is pressing to recover.

June 20, 2026 · Politics

Deputy PM Qubad Talabani says KDP and PUK are ready for a real agreement on the tenth KRG cabinet and offers to begin talks immediately

Deputy Prime Minister Qubad Talabani said on Saturday that the KDP and PUK have positive understandings toward a final cabinet-formation agreement and that he is "ready to meet right away" with the KDP to advance the process, marking the most emphatic PUK-side readiness signal since the parties resumed formal engagement in Sulaimani earlier this month.
