Damascus says SDF integration is in its final stages as an Autonomous Administration decision nears

Mustafa Abdi, a member of the presidential team implementing the integration accord with the Kurdish-led forces, told state media the folding of the Syrian Democratic Forces into state institutions has reached its final stages at the military and security levels, fueling expectations that a formal move to dissolve the SDF and the Autonomous Administration is close. Iraq

August 2, 2026 · Security High impact

Iran says the Oman-brokered Hormuz talks have reached their final stages

Iran's foreign minister said negotiations with Oman over a new arrangement for the Strait of Hormuz are in their final stages, the clearest sign yet of a path to reopening the waterway that carries the bulk of Iraq's crude and roughly 90 percent of its budget revenue. Talks have stuck on Tehran's demand for a transit fee regime, which Oman rejects and Washington calls a non-starter, so a deal that frees Iraqi tankers is close but not sealed. A June ceasefire collapsed within weeks over the same shipping dispute.

Al Jazeera
August 1, 2026 · Energy

Iraq and Turkey sign a one-year Ceyhan pipeline deal to route crude around the shut strait

Baghdad and Ankara signed a one-year agreement on Saturday to lift exports through the Kirkuk-Ceyhan pipeline to Turkey's Mediterranean coast, formalizing the northern route as Iraq's main outlet while Hormuz stays closed and the two sides negotiate a wider energy, power and water framework. Southern shipments through the Gulf have fallen to a fraction of the roughly 3.5 million barrels a day Iraq moved before the February war, making the reactivated line the country's lifeline for now.

Kuwait

August 2, 2026 · Energy

OPEC+ clears a sixth straight output rise for September then pauses into the fourth quarter

The eight OPEC+ producers unwinding voluntary cuts, Kuwait among them, agreed on Sunday to add another 188,000 barrels a day in September, a sixth consecutive monthly increase, but signaled a halt to further hikes in the fourth quarter with roughly two million barrels a day of cuts still held back. The group set its next review for September 6, keeping supply policy cautious as the Iran war clouds the demand outlook.

August 2, 2026 · Public Finance

Kuwait's fresh quota is stranded behind Hormuz as the break-even towers over the market

The September increase lands as barrels on paper for Kuwait, whose crude is entirely dependent on Hormuz and cannot sail while the strait stays shut, leaving the fiscal squeeze to deepen against a break-even near 90.5 dollars a barrel and a 2026-27 budget that pencils in a deficit of about 9.8 billion dinars. Storage limits mean a prolonged closure wipes out most of the country's output rather than banking it for higher prices later.

Kurdistan's fields carry the load as Iraq's northern export lifeline

With the reactivated Kirkuk-Ceyhan line now the country's main outlet under the fresh one-year deal with Turkey, Kurdistan Region crude channeled through SOMO is doing outsized work in keeping Iraqi barrels moving while Hormuz stays closed. A KRG delegation to Baghdad has kept up the push on public-sector salaries and the Region's entitlements even as it backs Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi, with Erbil arguing the federal government has covered only a fraction of the dues owed over recent years.
