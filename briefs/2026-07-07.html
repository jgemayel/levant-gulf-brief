Syria

July 6, 2026 · Diplomacy High impact

Macron lands in Damascus as the first Western European head of state to visit post-Assad Syria

French President Emmanuel Macron arrived in Damascus on July 6 for the first visit by a Western European head of state since Assad was toppled in December 2024, travelling with a delegation of French companies and investors and meeting President Ahmad al-Sharaa. Macron pressed for a free and pluralist Syria and for progress on security cooperation and Israel-Syria de-escalation, in a trip Damascus cast as the capstone of a fast-moving rehabilitation of ties with Paris after years of isolation.

The National
July 5, 2026 · Governance

Syria's first post-Assad parliament is complete on paper but its opening session slips

Al-Sharaa finalized the 210-seat People's Assembly by naming his one-third share of 70 members, among them 15 women and 13 figures previously jailed under Assad, with the remaining 140 chosen through an electoral-college system. The inaugural session set for July 6 was then postponed to an unannounced date by the head of the higher elections committee, a delay that coincided with the Macron visit and left the transitional legislature stood up but not yet sitting.

Arab News
July 6, 2026 · Security

Southern Syria stays tense as Daraa villagers block roads against Israeli incursions

Residents of villages in western Daraa closed roads to keep Israeli forces out as Israel kept up strikes and held buffer positions inside southern Syria, a standoff that shadowed Macron's push for de-escalation and the new government's efforts to project authority. The friction underscores that Damascus has yet to secure a credible arrangement to halt Israeli operations along the frontier even as its diplomatic standing recovers.

Iraq

July 5, 2026 · Energy

Iraq hands Halliburton two Basra fields and clears HKN for Hamrin, tilting upstream toward US firms

Basra Oil Company signed a five-year integrated management contract with Halliburton on July 5 to develop the Bin Omar and Sindbad fields, targeting crude of about 150,000 barrels a day at Bin Omar and 80,000 to 100,000 at Sindbad, while the cabinet also approved a deal for HKN Energy to operate the Hamrin field in Salahaddin. The awards are the clearest sign yet of Baghdad's push to draw US investment into its oil sector and cut reliance on Chinese, Russian and European operators, part of a drive toward 6 million barrels a day of capacity by 2028.

July 2026 · Diplomacy

Al-Zaidi frames his Washington trip as a new era, pitching an oil-backed development fund outside OPEC quotas

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi described his coming visit to Washington as the launch of a new era in US-Iraq relations, saying talks will feature a proposed Energy and Development Fund financed first by 500,000 barrels a day and potentially scaling to 2 million barrels a day of output kept outside OPEC production quotas. He tied the fund to a target of lifting Iraqi production toward 7 million barrels a day within three years, the revenue argument behind the recent run of US energy deals.

July 5, 2026 · Energy

Iraq's June seaborne crude exports jump to 488,000 barrels a day as Hormuz traffic returns

Iraqi seaborne crude shipments rose about 400 percent in June to roughly 488,000 barrels a day, up from around 98,000 in May, as maritime traffic through the Strait of Hormuz gradually resumed after the US-Iran agreement. The rebound still leaves exports more than 85 percent below the 3.33 million barrels a day averaged a year earlier, a gap that keeps oil revenue deeply depressed and frames Baghdad's rush to add barrels and new investment.

Kuwait

July 2026 · Energy

Kuwait leads the Gulf output rebound as OPEC production climbs back from a multi-decade low

Kuwait and Iran drove the biggest single-month gains as the 11 OPEC members lifted output to 19.43 million barrels a day in June, up 3.3 million from a May level that was the lowest in the survey's records back to 2000. Kuwait, whose production had crashed to about 573,000 barrels a day in May when the Hormuz shutdown severed its only export route, has rebuilt toward an estimated 1.65 million barrels a day and is steering back toward the 2 million mark, though Gulf supply remains well short of prewar normal.

OilPrice
July 2026 · Fiscal

The output recovery still leaves Kuwait's budget far under water at a $90 breakeven

With Brent back near its prewar baseline around $72 and Kuwait's fiscal breakeven at about $90.5 a barrel, the rebound in barrels does little to close a 2026/2027 budget that projects a KD 9.8 billion deficit, roughly $32 billion and the third largest since 1991. Wages and subsidies absorb about 76 percent of spending, and the finance ministry had already cut its budgeted oil price to $57 a barrel, leaving the recovery well below the level needed to balance the books.

Kurdistan

July 2026 · Fiscal

Erbil and Baghdad close on an ASYCUDA customs accord as the KRG presses for June salaries

A KRG delegation led by Council of Ministers Diwan chief Omed Sabah is in Baghdad nearing a final accord on the ASYCUDA customs system while pushing for the release of June public-sector salaries, with the two sides still split over customs classifications and how much of the digitized revenue Erbil keeps. The talks with the federal finance ministry and Federal Board of Supreme Audit are the mechanism through which the revived oil-for-salaries arrangement is being kept alive month to month.

Kurdistan24
July 2026 · Oil & Gas High impact

A July 27 pipeline treaty cliff threatens the Kurdistan Region's northern crude lifeline

The 1973 treaty governing the Iraq-Turkey Pipeline expires on July 27 after Ankara declined to renew it, and because federal and KRG barrels move under the same agreement, both would lose the Ceyhan route on that date unless Baghdad and Ankara strike a replacement. The deadline hangs over Kurdish exports that have climbed back above 200,000 barrels a day, with Iraq's oil ministry, the primary negotiator on the federal side, racing to draft new terms as Turkey seeks to settle a $1.5 billion arbitration award through in-kind energy rather than cash.
