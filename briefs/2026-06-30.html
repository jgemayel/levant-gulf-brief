Syria

June 29, 2026 · Security

Israeli troops push into Daraa's Yarmouk Basin and shell the village of Abidin, forcing families to flee overnight

Israeli forces advanced into the village of Abidin in the Yarmouk Basin of western Daraa on June 29 and, after residents tried to block the patrol with stones, answered with artillery that drove families to nearby villages through the night. Damascus condemned the raid and appealed for UN action while Turkey called it a violation of Syrian sovereignty, as the monitoring group Sijil counted close to 300 Israeli operations across Daraa and Quneitra in June alone, including 107 incursions and raids.

June 29, 2026 · Diplomacy

Syria's foreign minister makes his first Beirut visit, courting Lebanon's leaders to reset ties frayed under Assad

Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani held his first official talks in Beirut on June 29, meeting President Joseph Aoun, Prime Minister Nawaf Salam, and Speaker Nabih Berri before sitting with Druze leader Walid Jumblatt and Lebanese Forces chief Samir Geagea. The agenda ran across border security, detainees, and economic coordination, the most senior Syrian outreach to Lebanon since the transition and a bid to rebuild a relationship strained through years of Assad-era interference.

Iraq

June 28, 2026 · Security High impact

Iraq detains 47 officials, including lawmakers and a deputy oil minister, in its largest anti-graft sweep yet

Elite counterterrorism units raided Baghdad's fortified Green Zone and other districts before dawn on June 28 and arrested 47 officials, among them sitting members of parliament and Ali Maarej, the deputy oil minister for distribution, in the broadest corruption crackdown of Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's young tenure. The roundup drew on confessions from a deputy oil minister detained last month and put force behind al-Zaidi's twin pledges to fight graft and pull weapons under state control ahead of his mid-July visit to Washington.

Al Jazeera
June 29, 2026 · Energy

Al-Zaidi unveils a 400 billion dollar energy fund and a 7 million barrel target before his Washington trip

Ahead of his White House visit, al-Zaidi laid out an Energy and Development Fund he projects will gather roughly 400 billion dollars over three decades from between 500,000 and 2 million barrels a day of dedicated exports, alongside a goal of lifting Iraq's production capacity to 7 million barrels a day within three years. He directed the oil, electricity, and communications ministries to favor established US firms, framing the fund as the financial backbone of a wider energy realignment toward Washington.

Iraqi News
June 29, 2026 · Diplomacy

Iraq's foreign minister visits Damascus for the first time since Assad, agreeing to revive the cross-border oil pipeline

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Fuad Hussein made the first Iraqi ministerial trip to Damascus since Assad's fall on June 29, meeting President Ahmed al-Sharaa, counterpart Asaad al-Shaibani, and Syrian Energy Minister Mohammad al-Bashir. The two governments issued a joint statement to widen cooperation in energy, security, and the economy, agreed to rehabilitate the Iraq-to-Syria oil pipeline, and stood up a foreign-minister-led high committee to follow through, building on the fuel oil Baghdad began shipping through Syria in April.

Kuwait

June 30, 2026 · Labor

Kuwait closes its one-off window for moving workers out of restricted sectors as the June 30 deadline lands

June 30 is the final day for employers to shift foreign workers out of restricted sectors under an exceptional measure the Public Authority for Manpower opened on May 1, covering small and medium enterprises, industry, agriculture, livestock, and fishing. Transfers run only through the As'hal portal and need the original sponsor's approval, a temporary loosening the authority framed as a way to steady the labor market amid shifting economic conditions.

Arab Times
June 28, 2026 · Civil affairs

Kuwait's civil information authority cancels 464 residential addresses and warns of 100 dinar fines

The Public Authority for Civil Information struck 464 registered residential addresses from its records in a decision published in the official gazette on June 28, giving those affected 30 days to file new details through the Sahel app or face fines of up to 100 dinars under the 1982 civil information law. PACI said the cancellations followed property-owner declarations and the demolition of buildings tied to the listed addresses.

Kurdistan

June 29, 2026 · Energy

Baghdad hands the KRG a written guarantee to shield its oil fields as exports prepare to climb past 205,000 barrels a day

A joint federal-regional security committee finished assessing Kurdistan's oil fields and submitted its report to Prime Minister al-Zaidi, who gave international operators a written guarantee that Baghdad will protect the fields and their workers after the wartime drone strikes. Companies expect exports to rise above 205,000 barrels a day in the coming days, with firms splitting into those needing up to a year to repair drone damage, those restarting within weeks, and others still negotiating their place in the Erbil-Baghdad deal.

The KRG courts Spanish capital at a new economic forum, pitching tax breaks and full foreign ownership

Erbil used a Kurdistan Region-Spain Economic Forum on June 29 to chase fresh European investment, offering tax breaks and full foreign ownership while reaffirming a diversification drive that officials say has drawn more than 80 billion dollars across two decades. The Spain event extends a run of forums with Italy, France, and the Netherlands meant to widen the region's investment base beyond oil.
