Syria

June 26, 2026 · Energy

Damascus signs a wave of Saudi oilfield-services deals to revive production, targeting a 25 percent gas gain within six months

The state-owned Syrian Petroleum Company signed agreements with Saudi firms led by oilfield-services group ADES to redevelop existing fields, with the SPC chief saying the work should lift gas output about 25 percent within six months and as much as 50 percent by the end of 2026. The deals, which the company expects to create roughly 2,000 technical jobs, land the same week Syrian engineers recommissioned a workover rig in the central Homs fields, extending a Gulf-funded push to rebuild a hydrocarbon sector that is meant to drive a forecast 149 percent jump in 2026 public revenue.

SANA
June 25, 2026 · Aviation

Syrian Airlines readies its first European route in years, with a Damascus to Amsterdam flight set for July 2

Syria's civil aviation authority said the national carrier will resume direct Damascus to Amsterdam service on July 2, its first European destination since the EU lifted a ban on Syrian Arab Airlines accessing the bloc's airports in early 2025. Officials called Amsterdam the opening leg of a wider international expansion and said talks with German authorities on restoring Syria to Germany flights were expected to conclude in the first half of July.

Iraq

June 26, 2026 · Security High impact

Iran-backed factions parade new combat drones at Camp Ashraf, testing al-Zaidi's pledge to bring arms under state control

The Popular Mobilization Forces staged their largest military parade to date at Camp Ashraf in Diyala on June 26, marking the body's twelfth anniversary as units tied to Badr, Kataib Hezbollah, and Asaib Ahl al-Haq rolled out an array of Iranian-supplied systems. The standout reveal was the first public display of the Iranian Mohajer-6 armed drone in PMF service, a show of force that cuts directly against new Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's promise to confine weapons to the state and lands as Asaib-linked lawmakers revive a push to pass the stalled PMF service and retirement law.

Kuwait

June 2026 · Energy

Kuwait floats a 1.2 billion dinar drilling package as it pushes output back toward pre-war capacity

Kuwait Petroleum Corporation and its subsidiaries floated 25 major tenders worth more than 1.216 billion dinars to accelerate drilling, exploration, and production, anchored by a roughly 951 million dinar contract for directional and measurement-while-drilling services on unconventional wells. The program backs plans to drill 258 wells in the 2025-26 fiscal year, with Baker Hughes, Halliburton, Weatherford, and Schlumberger units competing, as KPC works to restore capacity after the wartime Strait of Hormuz shutdown.

Kurdistan

June 24, 2026 · Economy

KRG cabinet ties its customs system to Baghdad's and sets a 500,000 dinar floor for private-sector pensions

The KRG Council of Ministers, chaired by Prime Minister Masrour Barzani with Deputy Prime Minister Qubad Talabani present, endorsed a joint memorandum with Iraq's Federal Customs Authority to roll out the ASYCUDA electronic customs system across the region, a step Erbil framed as unifying procedures, digitizing border revenue, and tightening transparency. In the same session ministers set the minimum monthly private-sector pension at 500,000 Iraqi dinars and approved a reform package to reactivate the Social Security Fund and restore employer contributions.
