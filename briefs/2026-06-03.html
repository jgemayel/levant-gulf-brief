Trump folds Iraq into Tom Barrack's special-envoy mandate, merging the Damascus and Baghdad files under a single channel after Rubio closes the standalone Syria envoy slot

President Trump on Sunday named US Ambassador to Türkiye Tom Barrack as Special Presidential Envoy to Syria and Iraq, the day after Secretary Rubio formally ended Barrack's standalone Syria envoy mandate, expanding his diplomatic remit across both files while he keeps the Ankara post. The merge collapses the separate Syria and Iraq diplomatic channels into a single envoy line into Damascus and Baghdad and is being read across the region as a structural shift toward treating Iraq through a regional-balance lens rather than the post-2003 bilateral playbook.

Iraq

June 2, 2026 · Security High impact

Asaib Ahl al-Haq and Kataib al-Imam Ali both announce a full PMF break and weapons handover to PM al-Zaidi, with a four-member committee under Talibawi running the inventory

Asaib Ahl al-Haq announced on Tuesday it will sever ties with the Popular Mobilisation Forces and hand all of its weapons to the state, framing the decision as a response to a call from Grand Ayatollah al-Sistani and Coordination Framework pressure, with a four-member central committee chaired by Hajj Jawad al-Talibawi and including Rafid Saleh Ali, Abdullah Shakir Kamil and Ali Hamza Kadhim running the personnel, weapons, vehicles and logistics inventory directly with PM al-Zaidi. Kataib al-Imam Ali matched the move the same day, taking the running tally to AAH, Imam Ali, Sayid al-Shuhada, Ansar Allah al-Awfiya, Badr and Saraya al-Salam, with Kataib Hezbollah and Harakat al-Nujaba holding out and tying any handover to the foreign-forces question. AAH currently runs PMF Brigades 41, 42 and 43 alongside its Sadiqoon parliamentary bloc.
