May 6, 2026 · Central Bank

Central Bank of Syria unveils a 2026-2030 strategy with 30+ programs across five pillars and a global financial integration mandate

Central Bank Governor Abdulkader Husrieh launched a five-year strategy on Wednesday with more than 30 programs and initiatives organized around five pillars, confidence in the Syrian pound, a transparent FX market, financial sector integrity, digital payment infrastructure, and global financial integration. Husrieh framed the document as a clear roadmap for the institution's work and tied it to the broader monetary stability and economic recovery push that has been running through the QNB card-rails authorization, the new banknote rollout and the Berlin and Frankfurt central-bank tracks.
