QNB goes live as the first international card-network operator in Syria, one day after the Central Bank's perimeter decision

Qatar National Bank Group rolled out digital payment and international card acceptance services in Syria on Tuesday, with senior executive vice president for retail banking Adel Ali Al-Malki framing QNB as the first lender globally to support international card payments inside the Syrian market following the Central Bank of Syria authorization issued Monday. Al-Malki described a phased deployment in coordination with multiple stakeholders, working to local regulations and international standards, and tied the launch to Syria's broader push to rebuild monetary tools and restore international financial connections.

The Interior Ministry announced it foiled a large-scale terrorist plot and rolled up a Hezbollah-linked cell in coordinated operations across Damascus, Aleppo, Homs, Tartus and Latakia, arresting the operative the ministry said is directly responsible for the cell's assassinations file targeting senior Syrian officials. Iraq

May 5, 2026 · Government formation High impact

Federal Supreme Court takes the first formal challenge to al-Zaidi's PM mandate on four constitutional grounds

Former parliament member Raed al-Maliki, acting as a private citizen, filed a Federal Supreme Court lawsuit on Tuesday challenging Ali al-Zaidi's nomination as PM-designate on four grounds, alleged failure to meet candidacy requirements, constitutional and legal conflict of interest, procedural defects in being nominated by a political alliance rather than a parliamentary bloc, and the risk that disconnecting the candidacy from popular will depresses turnout and enables foreign interference. The court has not set a hearing date, but the filing puts a legal cloud over the cabinet submission Zaidi has slated for the start or middle of next week and gives any anti-Zaidi current inside the Coordination Framework, the Sunni track or the KRG a procedural lever it did not have on Monday.

The US State Department published a USD 10 million Rewards for Justice bounty for information on Harakat Hezbollah al-Nujaba leader Akram al-Kaabi, citing HAN attacks on US diplomatic facilities in Iraq and US bases in Iraq and Syria that killed a contractor and wounded servicemembers. Sudani cabinet settles 2024-25 and 2025-26 wheat dues and allocates 400,000 tons to KRG after the Tahrir Square crackdown

The cabinet approved settlement of farmers' wheat dues for the 2024-25 and 2025-26 agricultural seasons, deferred land-lease and irrigation-fee collection, directed the Finance Ministry to absorb loan principal and accrued interest into the federal budget, and allocated 400,000 tons of wheat to the Kurdistan Region. The Kuwaiti cabinet, chaired by PM Sheikh Ahmed Abdullah Al-Sabah, endorsed a new maritime law and reaffirmed full Kuwaiti backing for any UAE measures to defend its security and sovereignty after the Iranian missile and drone strikes, with Deputy PM Shereed Al-Maousherji delivering the readout. Komala Toilers logs over 70 Iranian drone and missile strikes on its Erbil and Sulaimani bases since the February war start

Komala Toilers of Kurdistan said Iranian forces have run more than 70 drone and missile attacks on its six camps in Erbil and Sulaimani provinces since the February 28 war start, killing at least one member and wounding five, and that strikes have continued despite the Pakistani-brokered ceasefire extended indefinitely on April 21. Regional data attributed to the spokesperson puts total cross-border strikes inside the Kurdistan Region at 809 between late February and April 20, with 20 deaths and 123 injuries, and counts a separate 114 strikes against KDPI bases in the same window.

KRG anti-violence directorate logs 12,456 domestic-violence complaints in 2025 and presses for new statutory protections
