Syria

July 25, 2026 · Diplomacy

Guterres closes his Damascus visit with an appeal for the world and a rebuke of Israel

UN Secretary General Antonio Guterres wrapped the first visit to Syria by a UN chief since 2009 by urging the international community to stand behind the Syrian people and fund recovery, while sharply criticizing Israel's continued military actions in the south. His time in Damascus backed President Ahmed al-Sharaa's push for faster aid and a fuller role for the UN disengagement force along the Golan.

The Washington Times
July 25, 2026 · Security

Israeli troops enter al-Samdaniyah al-Sharqiyah as raids across Quneitra widen

An Israeli force of five vehicles pushed into al-Samdaniyah al-Sharqiyah in Quneitra, set up a temporary checkpoint, searched residents and fired a mortar round and a smoke shell before withdrawing, with no casualties reported. Damascus condemned the raid as part of near-daily incursions across the 1974 disengagement line and renewed its demand for a full Israeli pullback from southern Syria.

Iraq

July 25, 2026 · Diplomacy High impact

US holds fire on Iran for a second day as a Hormuz compromise takes shape

The US military held off striking Iran for a second straight day, its first pause in nearly two weeks, as mediators pushed both sides back toward the interim ceasefire and Oman-Iran talks advanced on reopening the Strait of Hormuz. A deal taking shape would let Iran manage vessel transit through the strait with lighter restrictions, a shift that could begin to unlock Iraq's southern exports, which have stayed near 1.3 million barrels a day, roughly 70 percent below normal, with tankers still shunning the Gulf.

AP via Las Vegas Sun
July 24, 2026 · Economy

Brent slides back under 100 dollars as the war premium cools

Brent crude slipped back below 100 dollars to about 96 on Friday, down roughly 4 percent on the week, as the pause in US strikes and progress toward a Hormuz compromise took some heat out of prices. The retreat eases pressure on Baghdad, which draws close to 90 percent of its budget from oil, even as its main southern shipping route stays largely shut and Houthi strikes keep the Red Sea alternative unsafe.

Kuwait

July 24, 2026 · Economy

Kuwait's crude output faces a quarter cut with no pipeline to skirt Hormuz

Kuwait's oil output could fall by as much as a quarter this month, sliding toward 1.2 million barrels a day from about 1.6 million in June, after the renewed escalation effectively re-shut the Strait of Hormuz, Rystad Energy estimated. Unlike Saudi Arabia and the UAE, Kuwait has no pipeline to bypass the waterway, leaving almost all of its crude exposed to the closure and any forced production cutback.

July 25, 2026 · Security

A fragile lull settles over Kuwait's skies as the US strike pause holds

Kuwait saw a quieter weekend as the US pause in strikes appeared to slow the missile and drone waves that had struck the country for much of the past two weeks, including a claimed Iranian hit on the Ali al-Salem air base on Thursday. Iran's army warned it would resume attacks the moment it came under fire again, leaving the calm tentative and Kuwait's defenses on alert.

Kurdistan

July 24, 2026 · Energy

Khor Mor shutdown now strips 30 percent of Erbil's water on top of its power

The halt at the Khor Mor gas field has cut Erbil's water supply by about 30 percent, compounding a power loss of up to 80 percent after drone strikes forced Dana Gas to stop production, with no timeline yet for a restart. Water stations that depend on the field's electricity have scaled back pumping as the Region absorbs the loss of roughly 2,500 to 3,000 megawatts.

July 24, 2026 · Economy

Cooking gas doubles across the Region as the KRG trucks in emergency supply

Household cooking gas has roughly doubled since the Khor Mor halt, with a standard cylinder refill now near 18,000 dinars against about 9,000 before mid-July and LPG at filling stations up to 800 dinars a liter. The KRG is trucking gas across the Region to head off outright shortages while Dana Gas keeps the field offline for worker safety, with production still not restored.
