Syria

May 17, 2026 · Fiscal

Sharaa issues Decree 117 of 2026 waiving customs-violation fines on pre-December 2024 cases under a six-month settlement window

President Ahmad al-Sharaa issued Decree 117 of 2026 on Sunday exempting customs violators from fines under Articles 253 to 278 of the 2006 Customs Law for cases that occurred before December 8, 2024, provided settlement of duties and taxes is completed within six months, with collected revenues routed to the state treasury and narcotics-related cases and finalised judicial rulings carved out. The decree pulls a long backlog of pre-transition customs cases into a defined revenue-collection window and is the latest in the post-Assad presidency's run of administrative-and-fiscal cleanup decrees that has also restructured the national import-export committee on the same day.

Iraq

May 16, 2026 · Cabinet High impact

Zaidi sworn in with a partial 14-minister cabinet, Khudair takes Oil and al-Sari takes Finance, first cabinet session orders one-week asset disclosures and a 30-day priorities map

Parliament confirmed PM-designate Ali Falih Kadhim al-Zaidi on Thursday in a 70-minute session with 270 votes for 14 of 23 ministers, elevating former deputy Bassim Mohammed Khudair to the Oil Ministry, naming Faleh al-Sari to Finance and retaining Fuad Hussein at Foreign Affairs, while leaving Interior, Defence, Higher Education, Planning, Housing, Labour, Reconstruction, Education and a small-portfolio cluster to a post-Eid vote. At the first cabinet session on Saturday, al-Zaidi ordered every minister to file financial disclosures within one week, set a 30-day window for each ministry to file priorities, and put electricity shortages, digital-transformation rollout and parliament-oversight coordination on the cabinet's near-term work list.
