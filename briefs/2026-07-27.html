Syria

July 26, 2026 · Diplomacy

Al-Sharaa says Syria is negotiating a security pact with Israel

President Ahmed al-Sharaa said Damascus is working with several countries toward a security agreement with Israel that would halt cross-border clashes and could open a path to a broader peace, in an interview aired July 26 on Al Jazeera's Al Muqabala. He said any deal would not surrender Syria's claim to the occupied Golan Heights and would center on Israeli forces leaving the buffer zone they seized after Assad's fall in 2024, even as near-daily Israeli raids continue across Quneitra.

Al-Sharaa rules out sending Syrian forces into Lebanon

In the same interview al-Sharaa ruled out any Syrian military intervention in Lebanon, rebuffing President Trump's suggestion of a Syrian role against Hezbollah and saying decisions of war and peace must rest with the Lebanese state. He cast Syria's approach to both Lebanon and Israel around economic cooperation and said the country is opening a new chapter in its regional and international standing after decades of isolation.

Iraq

July 27, 2026 · Energy

The 1973 Kirkuk to Ceyhan accord lapses today as Baghdad and Ankara race an interim deal

The 1973 agreement governing crude flows through the Kirkuk to Ceyhan pipeline expires July 27, and Ankara and Baghdad are finishing a 12-month interim protocol to keep oil moving while they negotiate a new framework, with Turkish Energy Minister Alparslan Bayraktar saying the deal is at its final stage and should be signed within days. The same line carries the roughly 230,000 barrels a day of Kurdish crude that Erbil routes through SOMO, so the protocol also decides whether KRG exports and the salaries they underpin keep flowing.

Türkiye Today
July 26, 2026 · Diplomacy High impact

US-Iran Hormuz truce snags on who pays to cross the strait

A US-Iran compromise to reopen the Strait of Hormuz has stalled over transit fees, with Tehran signaling it will charge ships once the 60-day free-passage window in the June memorandum ends while President Trump insists the strait stay toll free, even as mediators float a 10-day ceasefire to steady the truce. Traffic remains choked at about 25 tankers a day, far below the pre-war norm, keeping Iraq's southern exports throttled well below capacity for a country that draws close to 90 percent of its budget from oil.

Kuwait

July 26, 2026 · Security

Kuwait writes its defense pact with Pakistan into law

Kuwait published Decree-Law 73 of 2026 ratifying a defense cooperation agreement with Pakistan, covering military training, logistics, personnel exchanges, defense technology and intelligence, with a joint military committee set to meet yearly. Officials stressed the pact is about capacity building for Kuwaiti forces and carries no mutual defense guarantee, unlike the strategic accord Islamabad recently signed with Saudi Arabia.

Kurdistan

July 26, 2026 · Energy

Khor Mor is back online and Kurdistan's grid is recovering

Dana Gas has restarted production at the Khor Mor field after weeks offline, and the Kurdistan Region's power supply is stabilizing as gas returns to the plants that generate most of its electricity. The halt had stripped up to 80 percent of the Region's power and roughly 2,500 to 3,000 megawatts of capacity, cut Erbil's water supply by about 30 percent and doubled cooking gas prices before the resumption.

Barzani presses parties to seat a cabinet and keep the Region out of the war

KRG Prime Minister Masrour Barzani urged Kurdish parties to reopen the regional parliament and form the long-delayed new cabinet without further delay, warning that the Kurdistan Region should not be dragged into the regional conflict as a target since it threatens no one. US envoy Tom Barrack echoed the call to reactivate parliament and complete the government formation stalled since the last regional election.
