Syria

August 24, 2026 · Sanctions High impact

Washington removes Syria from the state sponsor of terrorism list after 47 years

The US State Department formally rescinded Syria's designation as a state sponsor of terrorism, a label carried since 1979, with the move taking effect after the 45-day congressional review triggered when President Trump notified Congress on July 8. Treasury's OFAC simultaneously delisted Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham as a specially designated global terrorist, leaving only Cuba, Iran, and North Korea on the list. Damascus called it the removal of the last major obstacle to foreign investment and to rejoining the international financial system, with President Ahmed al-Sharaa hailing the decision as historic.

Al Jazeera
August 24, 2026 · Diplomacy

Syrian and Israeli delegations hold US-mediated talks in Jordan

A Syrian delegation led by Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani, joined by the general and military intelligence chiefs, met an Israeli team in Jordan to build mechanisms to halt Israeli strikes and revive talks on a security agreement, with al-Shaibani meeting Mossad chief Roman Gofman. The two sides also discussed keeping the Israel-Turkey rivalry off Syrian soil. Shaibani told Reuters he expects negotiations to resume soon, after contacts were cut following the August 18 Israeli strike on the Abu al-Duhur airbase, though Damascus signaled it will not accept limits on its right to build up its military.

A bomb hits an Internal Security Forces bus near Damascus and Gulf states condemn it

An explosive device detonated on a bus carrying Internal Security Forces personnel on the Marouneh-al-Tal road in the Damascus countryside, wounding several with no confirmed deaths. Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Jordan condemned the attack as terrorism, and analysts flagged a possible shift of ISIS activity toward urban areas. Iraq

August 24, 2026 · Sanctions High impact

Treasury launches Operation Economic Outcast on Iran, a squeeze routed through Iraq

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent rolled out the sanctions campaign he had billed an economic D-Day, issuing sectoral determinations against Iran's digital assets, technology, gold, aviation, and shipping while OFAC blacklisted more than 60 entities, individuals, and vessels tied to oil revenue and weapons procurement. Bessent warned no firm or country is beyond the reach of US secondary sanctions, a threat that lands hardest on Iraq, the main conduit for Iranian energy and financial flows. Brent fell about 2.5 percent to roughly 92 dollars a barrel as traders judged the package would not immediately cut physical Gulf flows.

Al Jazeera
August 23, 2026 · Politics

Baghdad recasts the September 30 deadline as a coalition exit, not a disarmament date

Prime ministerial security adviser Qasim al-Araji told local media that September 30 is the date for the US-led coalition to depart, not a deadline for factions to hand over their weapons, an implicit softening of Baghdad's June pledge to disarm armed groups by the end of the month. Reporting tied the shift to pressure from Iran-aligned factions and to the recent Baghdad visit of Iran's parliament speaker, even as Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi privately told lawmakers he has no intention of retreating. Officials reaffirmed the coalition pullout itself stays fixed for September 30.

August 24, 2026 · Diplomacy

Iraq's security adviser and the US chargé press the state monopoly on arms

National Security Adviser Qasim al-Aboudi met US Chargé d'Affaires Joshua Harris in Baghdad to discuss bilateral security cooperation, regional de-escalation, and the drive to confine all weapons to state control. Al-Aboudi reaffirmed the government's commitment to consolidating arms under official command, framing the effort as a way to steady the country and lift the economy. The meeting slots into the wider disarmament fight running against the September 30 backdrop.

Kuwait

August 24, 2026 · Politics

Kuwait strips naturalized citizens of the vote and candidacy by decree

Amiri Decree-Law No. 79 of 2026, published in the official gazette Kuwait Al-Youm, bars Kuwaitis who acquired nationality through naturalization from voting, running for office, or being appointed to any parliamentary body, ending a prior right to vote 30 years after naturalization. The decree also grants electronic citizenship certificates the same legal force as paper ones. It lands amid a mass citizenship-revocation drive that has stripped tens of thousands of people since 2024.

Kuwait Times
August 24, 2026 · Energy

Iran warns of fresh Hormuz seizures as Brent slips toward 92 dollars

Tehran warned it would seize vessels that violate its new transit rules in the Strait of Hormuz, timed to the Bessent sanctions push and following the August 17 lapse of the 60-day US-Iran safe-passage arrangement. The threat keeps the export artery for Kuwaiti, Iraqi, and Saudi crude under severe risk even as Brent eased about 2.5 percent to roughly 92 dollars a barrel. Kuwait's oil-dependent budget remains exposed to any further disruption of the waterway.

Kurdistan

August 23, 2026 · Business

A Baghdad court suspends the order to shut down Korek Telecom

Baghdad's Rusafa Court issued a stay suspending the federal Communications and Media Commission decision to cancel Korek Telecom's contract, shut down its network, and seize its assets, according to Korek chief executive Sirwan Barzani. The reversal averts, for now, a shutdown the KRG-based operator had warned would cut service to millions of subscribers, as Korek argues the commission overstepped its authority. The fight over alleged contractual breaches remains unresolved.

The New Region
August 23, 2026 · Politics

The Federal Supreme Court throws out five suits against the KRG over a 1992 property decree

Iraq's Federal Supreme Court issued final rulings rejecting five lawsuits, four brought by former finance minister Taif Sami alongside the heads of Rafidain Bank, Al-Rasheed Bank, and the National Insurance Company, that sought to annul a November 1992 KRG decree granting the Region ownership of Iraqi government properties inside Kurdistan. The court dismissed all five on jurisdiction grounds without ruling on the merits, a procedural win for Erbil against Baghdad institutions.

August 23, 2026 · Economy

Erbil moved 172 tons of gold and silver worth 885 million dollars so far this year

About 172 tons of gold and silver worth roughly 885 million dollars moved through Erbil, mainly its international airport, from January 1 to August 20, according to trading firm Dastan Gold International. Gold trade totaled 375 million dollars, split between 285 million in imports and 90 million in exports, while silver ran to about 170 tons worth 510 million dollars, all imports routed via UAE and Turkey hubs. The figures underline Erbil's role as a regional bullion transit point even through the war economy.
