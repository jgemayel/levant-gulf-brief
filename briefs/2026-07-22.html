Syria

July 21, 2026 · Diplomacy

Guterres to make the first UN chief visit to Syria since 2009

UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres will travel to Damascus later this week at the government's invitation, the first visit by a UN secretary-general since 2009, with meetings set with President Ahmed al-Sharaa and Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani. The trip follows a Security Council session that hailed Syria's political transition and urged support for recovery, and Guterres plans to stop at the UNDOF disengagement force in the south.

July 18, 2026 · Economy

Trucked Iraqi crude turns Syria into the Middle East's top fuel oil exporter

A fleet of thousands of trucks hauling Iraqi fuel oil to the Mediterranean port of Baniyas pushed Syria's fuel oil exports to 720,000 tons in June, about 28 percent of Middle East volumes and the region's largest single flow, according to Vortexa data. Each truck carries roughly 135 barrels on a four to six day drive as Baghdad reroutes supply away from the blockaded Strait of Hormuz. See the Iraq section for Baghdad's wider export scramble.

Israeli forces push into a Quneitra village and set up a checkpoint

Israeli troops entered al-Samdaniyah al-Sharqiyah in Quneitra province with five military vehicles, set up a temporary checkpoint, searched residents and fired a mortar round and a smoke shell with no reported casualties. Damascus condemned the raid as part of continuing incursions into the south and renewed its call for a full Israeli withdrawal. Translated from SANA.

July 20, 2026 · Security

Syria seizes 226 grenades at the Lebanon border in a second bust in a week

Customs officers at the Jdeidet Yabous crossing near Damascus found 226 RPG-type grenades hidden in a vehicle bound for Lebanon and arrested the driver, the second interdiction of a Hezbollah-bound arms shipment in under a week after the July 16 seizure of Iranian missiles and drones. The busts underscore how sharply post-Assad Damascus has turned against the old Iran-to-Hezbollah supply line.

Damascus sets a 2027 target to close every displacement camp

The government unveiled a coordinated plan to end camp-based displacement by 2027, phasing out tents during 2026 through infrastructure repair, support for voluntary returns and cooperation with international partners. The initiative lands as Syria courts widening Western and Gulf re-engagement a year and a half after Assad's fall.

Iraq

July 20, 2026 · War High impact

US intensifies strikes on Iran as mediators float a 10-day Hormuz truce

The United States carried out fresh strikes on Iran and moved more fighter jets into the region after President Trump said Tehran would pay for the deaths of three US service members in recent days. Mediators floated a 10-day ceasefire that would also lift restrictions on the Strait of Hormuz, though Washington played down the prospect, and Brent crude held near 88 dollars, up about 16 percent on the week. Iraq hosts the US troops in the crosshairs and ships most of its crude through the strait, leaving it squarely inside the blast radius.

CNN
July 20, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Iraq's July crude exports climbed to 1.5 million barrels a day before the latest escalation

Oil Minister Bassem Mohammed Khudair said shipments through Hormuz and the Ceyhan pipeline averaged about 1.5 million barrels a day in July before the newest round of strikes, roughly three times some June estimates. Around 90 percent of Iraq's 3.4 million barrels a day of exports still moves through the contested strait, leaving Baghdad exposed as it races to build overland alternatives through Syria, Jordan and Turkey.

July 19, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Markets bet on a post-Hormuz oil map as ships shun the US-backed route

Traders increasingly expect new Middle East pipeline capacity to offset most Hormuz volumes within a few years, even as vessels reject the US-backed alternate corridor that Baghdad and Washington are promoting. Goldman Sachs estimates enough pipeline capacity could be added to insulate about 75 percent of pre-war Gulf exports by the end of 2028, a slow cushion for producers hurting now.

Kuwait

July 20, 2026 · War High impact

Iran's sixth Nasr-2 wave targets Camp Arifjan as Kuwait intercepts the barrage

Kuwait's General Staff said its air defenses intercepted a fresh missile and drone attack on July 20, with the IRGC claiming it struck US naval commando facilities and HIMARS launchers at Camp Arifjan in a sixth wave of Operation Nasr 2. Kuwait said the explosions heard across the country were its own interceptions, and no independent confirmation of the Iranian damage claims was available.

July 19, 2026 · Security

Iranian strikes damage a Kuwaiti oil facility and power and water plants

Repeated Iranian attacks caused significant damage at a Kuwait Petroleum Corporation oil facility, a power generation plant, a water desalination plant and several residential areas, with some injuries reported, as Tehran widened its target list to pressure Washington. The strikes deepen the strain on a state that draws nearly all its revenue from oil and most of its drinking water from desalination.

Kurdistan

July 20, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Turkey refuses to renew the Kirkuk to Ceyhan pipeline deal days before it expires

Ankara told Baghdad it will not extend the 2010 Kirkuk to Ceyhan pipeline agreement under its old terms when it expires on July 27, citing unresolved arbitration disputes and pressing for fuller use and new connections. Iraq and Turkey are preparing a temporary one-year protocol to keep crude flowing through Ceyhan, the northern artery that has become the Region's and Iraq's main Hormuz bypass. See the Iraq section for the wider export picture.

July 21, 2026 · KRG Fiscal

Baghdad transfers 957 billion dinars for the Region's July salaries

The KRG finance ministry said Baghdad sent nearly 957 billion dinars to its Central Bank of Iraq account for July public sector wages, with digital disbursement to more than 501,500 card holders beginning over three days, covering 266,400 civil servants, 191,500 Peshmerga and internal security members and 43,600 pensioners. The payout eases a chronic standoff that left 2025 salaries funded for only ten of twelve months.

July 20, 2026 · Oil & Gas

Technical and legal snags keep the Region's own oil exports stalled

KRG officials said unresolved technical, financial and legal obstacles, compounded by drone damage to fields, continue to delay a full restart of the Region's independent oil exports, with no firm timetable set. The delay leaves Erbil leaning on federal transfers and the shared Ceyhan route rather than its own crude sales.
