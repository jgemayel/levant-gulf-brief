US envoy warns Israel's Syrian airbase strike risked a clash with Turkey

US ambassador to Turkey and Syria envoy Tom Barrack said the Israeli strike on the Abu al-Duhur airbase in Idlib could have baited Ankara into a direct military confrontation, since Turkish forces had no warning the aircraft were inbound and might have scrambled jets believing they were under attack. Barrack called the raid an unnecessary escalation that does not advance regional stability and said Israel gave Washington no advance notice, sharpening friction over Turkey's plan to rehabilitate the long-idle base.

Iraq

August 22, 2026 · Energy High impact

Iran lets some Iraqi oil tankers through the Strait of Hormuz

Iraqi President Nizar Amidi said Iran has facilitated the passage of some vessels carrying Iraqi crude through the Strait of Hormuz in recent days, a breakthrough after months in which the near-total closure choked exports from a country that pumped about 4 million barrels a day before the war and owns no national tanker fleet. Special permission for Iraqi tankers was a main Baghdad ask during Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Baqer Qalibaf's visit this week, and Amidi stressed that Iraqi soil will not be used to launch attacks on any other state.

August 22, 2026 · Energy

Iraq plans new export pipelines as August crude shipments hold near 2 million barrels a day

Baghdad is drawing up new pipeline routes to harden its export options after the Hormuz disruption, with daily shipments averaging about 2 million barrels through the first half of August, the strongest run since the war began. State marketer SOMO has set its September Basra loading program at roughly 3.017 million barrels a day, a signal that officials expect the recovery to extend as northern and alternative outlets carry more of the load.

Kuwait

August 22, 2026 · Energy

Kuwaiti and Gulf producers run a tanker shuttle to keep crude moving past Hormuz

Kuwaiti, Saudi, Qatari and Emirati oil firms have chartered tankers that switch off their transponders and dash from the Gulf to the Gulf of Oman under US Navy escort, a workaround that lifted Strait of Hormuz traffic about 27 percent in a week to 103 vessels inbound and 89 outbound. The improvised corridor has eased the squeeze on Kuwait, which cut output when the strait first closed in the spring.

August 22, 2026 · Diplomacy

Kuwait agrees to open access to Iranian pilots missing since the air war

Kuwait has agreed to facilitate access to four Iranian pilots unaccounted for since their aircraft were downed early in the conflict, Iran's semi-official Tasnim agency reported, as Tehran pressed Qatar to move other detained airmen to a hospital on land and asked the Red Cross to send an air ambulance. Three of the men, Javad Salehi, Abdul Majid Dashtian and Imran Behroshian, have been held for six months.

Kurdistan

August 21, 2026 · Energy

Baghdad, Erbil and operators renew the Ceyhan oil export deal into 2027

The federal government, the KRG and the region's producers renewed their tripartite agreement for pipeline exports to Turkey's Ceyhan port, extending it to January 27, 2027, after an interim Baghdad and Ankara arrangement kept crude moving past the expiry of the old Iraq-Turkey pipeline treaty. The northern route has taken on outsized weight with the Strait of Hormuz still choked, and Baghdad is now pushing Basra crude north through the KRG's network to add as much as 330,000 barrels a day to Ceyhan flows.
