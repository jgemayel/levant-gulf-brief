Syria

August 21, 2026 · Politics High impact

Mazloum Abdi declares the SDF's integration into the Syrian state complete

Speaking from Qamishli, SDF commander Mazloum Abdi said the merger of the Kurdish-led administration's military, security, and civil bodies into Syria's state institutions is finished, ending more than a decade of self-rule in the northeast. Tens of thousands of personnel now sit on government payrolls, with roughly 5,000 fighters folded into the Defense Ministry and about 4,000 internal security members moved to the Interior Ministry after retraining near Damascus. The step delivers on the January 14-point deal, though Kurdish demands for meaningful autonomy stay unresolved and Washington called the outcome historic.

Shibani briefs Ankara on the Kurdish integration and takes aim at Israel

Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shibani used a visit to Ankara to update Turkish officials on the completed SDF integration and to condemn Israel's recent airbase strike as a threat to Syria's recovery. Turkey, fresh off its own ceasefire with the PKK, wants SDF elements left outside the national army to demobilize and surrender their weapons in tandem with the PKK's dissolution, a demand that keeps the northeast file live even after the merger. Iraq

August 20, 2026 · Security

Baghdad takes its militia disarmament fight public before the September 30 deadline

With political talks stalled, Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's government has moved the disarmament campaign onto television and social media to build public backing to bring all weapons under state control by September 30. A powerful Iran-aligned faction warned that forcing the issue could trigger internal strife and set three conditions, a full US troop withdrawal, removal of strategic threats, and further development of Iraq's armed forces. Al-Zaidi is weighing decisive measures, among them sending security forces to retake Jurf al-Sakhr, which Kataib Hezbollah has controlled for over a decade.

Kuwait

August 20, 2026 · Energy

Kuwait's power grid holds through 49-degree heat despite a Salwa outage

The Ministry of Electricity, Water and Renewable Energy said a transformer failure at the main Salwa (A) substation knocked out power in parts of blocks 7 and 10 before crews restored supply, one of several localized cuts as temperatures neared 49 degrees Celsius. Officials said the national grid stayed stable near peak summer load and urged residents to trim consumption and replace old air conditioners, with demand pushing past 15,000 megawatts at the hottest hours.

Kurdistan

August 21, 2026 · Security

Barzani presses allies for air defenses as strikes on the KRG top a thousand

KRG Prime Minister Masrour Barzani appealed to international partners to help the Region acquire effective air defenses, warning that the drawdown of US forces and the redeployment of Patriot batteries have left it exposed as Iran-linked drone and missile fire mounts. A local tally counts more than 1,000 attacks on the KRG since the war began on February 28, including 16 drones and 3 missiles in the first two weeks of August alone, after the August 17 strike on his private office and the security council director's home. Barzani said no effective system is yet in place to counter the threat.
