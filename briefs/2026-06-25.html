Syria

June 24, 2026 · Security High impact

US airstrike kills senior ISIS leader Ali Husayn al-Ulaywi in northwest Syria as the group presses its campaign against al-Sharaa

US Central Command said on June 24 that a June 19 precision strike in northwest Syria killed Ali Husayn al-Ulaywi, a senior Islamic State figure, part of an intensifying effort to break up ISIS cells that have stepped up attacks on the new government. CENTCOM commander Admiral Brad Cooper said forces remain committed to rooting out the group's remnants, days after ISIS gunmen killed two Syrian soldiers near Manbij.

The Washington Post
June 22, 2026 · Diplomacy

UN Security Council reviews Syria's transition, with the envoy's team flagging Israeli incursions as the main drag on stability

At a June 22 Security Council briefing, deputy special envoy Claudio Cordone described a positive trajectory for Syria's transition while warning that political timelines have slipped and humanitarian needs remain acute. Syria's representative told the chamber that Israel is the main obstacle to stability and that its incursions are undermining the US-brokered Iran ceasefire.

Egypt condemns Israeli violations of Syrian sovereignty as Cairo and Damascus deepen a postwar thaw

On the sidelines of Arab League consultations in Amman on June 22, Egyptian Foreign Minister Badr Abdelatty met Syrian counterpart Asaad al-Shibani, condemned Israeli incursions, and called for a withdrawal in line with the 1974 disengagement accord. The two agreed to hold a second senior government meeting on trade and investment, a fresh marker of the Cairo-Damascus rapprochement.

June 23, 2026 · Economy

Lebanon-Syria sea passenger service resumes as the Cedar Waves docks at Latakia after years of suspension

The Cedar Waves passenger ship, run by Lebanon's Abou Merhi Agency, arrived at Latakia from Jounieh on June 22, restoring regular Lebanon-Syria sea travel that had been suspended for years. Summer sailings will run two to three times a week with fares around 125 to 139 euros, a small but tangible sign of normalization along the Levantine coast.

The National
June 24, 2026 · Humanitarian

FAO says 13.4 million Syrians face acute food insecurity and seeks 286 million dollars to steady farming

In a June 24 briefing, FAO acting representative Pirro Tomaso Perri said 13.4 million Syrians are food insecure and 30 percent of households cannot meet daily needs after wheat output fell about 60 percent across 2024 and 2025. The agency's 2026 to 2028 response plan seeks 286 million dollars to reach 9.8 million people, a sharper funding ask than its earlier hotspot warnings.

Iraq

June 24, 2026 · Markets High impact

Oil slides to pre-war lows as Hormuz traffic resumes, squeezing Iraq's thin budget buffers

Brent for August fell 3.05 percent to 74.73 dollars and US crude closed at 70.34 dollars on June 24, dipping below 70 for the first time since March 2, as tankers streamed back through the Strait of Hormuz and more than 11,000 stranded seafarers began exiting the Gulf. The IEA has named Iraq the major producer hardest hit by the war, leaving Baghdad to rebuild exports into a falling price, a squeeze shared by Kuwait in the next section.

CNBC
June 22, 2026 · Finance

Iraq installs Nazar Nasser Hussein as central bank governor in an FATF-driven shake-up

Nazar Nasser Hussein, the Central Bank of Iraq's former anti-money-laundering chief, took charge on June 22, replacing Ali al-Allaq, who moves to economic adviser to Prime Minister al-Zaidi. The change puts a compliance specialist atop the bank as Iraq works to exit the FATF grey list, with dozens of private banks still barred from dollar transactions and the parallel-market dinar near 1,550 to the dollar.

June 24, 2026 · Politics

Iraq's parliament set to convene July 5 to fill the defense and interior posts before al-Zaidi's Washington trip

The Al-Hikma Movement said parliament will sit on July 5 to vote on the nine remaining cabinet portfolios, including the contested interior and defense ministries, so al-Zaidi can present a complete government before his mid-July White House meeting with Trump. Government spokesman Haider al-Aboudi said the new ministers would be seated before the trip.

Kuwait

June 24, 2026 · Diplomacy

Kuwait's Emir hosts Rubio as Washington works to reassure the Gulf over the Iran deal

Emir Sheikh Meshal Al-Ahmad received US Secretary of State Marco Rubio at Bayan Palace on June 24, part of a Gulf tour through the UAE, Kuwait, and Bahrain. Rubio acknowledged Gulf unease that the US-Iran memorandum leaves out curbs on Iran's missile program and proxies and floats a 300 billion dollar reconstruction fund, telling reporters Washington would stay completely aligned with its Gulf partners, all of which were struck during the war.

Gulf News
June 24, 2026 · Economy

Kuwait amends the KPC law to strengthen its commercial model and competitiveness

Kuwait amended the Kuwait Petroleum Corporation law to bolster the state oil company's commercial footing, allowing future capital adjustments through a legislative mechanism while keeping the current board in place until its term ends, KUNA reported on June 24. The revision lands as KPC drives a fast postwar output recovery toward pre-war levels.

Kuwait Times
June 24, 2026 · Recovery

Oman Air restores Kuwait flights through Terminal 4 as the airport's phased recovery widens

Oman Air said it would resume Muscat to Kuwait flights from June 25 after a suspension in place since March, routing temporarily through Terminal 4 rather than the war-damaged Terminal 1. It joins Kuwait Airways, Qatar Airways, Etihad, and Emirates in the gradual return of service to Kuwait International Airport.

Kurdistan

June 24, 2026 · Oil & Gas

KRG oil companies restart production field by field as Gulf Keystone reopens Shaikan

KRG spokesman Peshawa Hawramani said on June 24 that Gulf Keystone had resumed output at Shaikan, one of the region's largest fields, with Norway's DNO due to restart Tawke and Peshkabir on June 27 and HKN to reopen Atrush on June 29, already running at about 7,000 barrels a day. Hunt Oil returns on July 8, completing a phased restart after wartime drone strikes forced a shutdown across the Kurdistan Region.

Shafaq News
June 24, 2026 · Fiscal

KRG finance ministry rejects a call to freeze federal transfers, saying the region met every legal obligation

The KRG Ministry of Finance pushed back on Iraqi parliament deputy speaker Adnan al-Dulaimi's call to suspend transfers to Erbil, saying the region kept sending Baghdad its share of non-oil revenue from January through May under Law 6 of 2019 and successive budget laws. It noted that March and April receipts fell sharply on wartime conditions and an unfinished customs rollout at KRG border crossings.

June 23, 2026 · Economy

KRG unveils a 24-project tourism investment roadmap with tax breaks and full foreign ownership

The Kurdistan Regional Government laid out 24 strategic tourism projects spread across its provinces, from Shaqlawa's Chneran resort in Erbil to an eco-resort on the Sirwan River in Sulaimani, offering pre-allocated land, infrastructure, tax exemptions, and full foreign ownership. The package is part of a drive to diversify the regional economy beyond oil revenue.
