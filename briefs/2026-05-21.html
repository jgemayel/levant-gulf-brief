May 20, 2026 · Investment

UAE's Eagle Hills crystallises two Syrian masterplans valued north of 50 billion dollars across Damascus Dummar and Latakia, with 73,000+ housing units and 3,200 hotel keys in the capital plan

Eagle Hills is studying two large urban development projects in Syria with a combined budget above 50 billion dollars, with the Damascus Dummar masterplan covering about 33 million square metres for more than 73,000 housing units, around 3,200 hotel rooms and 7 million square metres of green space, and the Latakia masterplan covering nearly 15 million square metres with more than 29,000 housing units and 2,800 hotel rooms. The developer projects cumulative GDP contribution above 18 billion dollars, foreign cash inflows of around 7.5 billion dollars and roughly 5.5 billion dollars in FDI over a ten-year economic-impact period, with 70,000 jobs in construction and 25,000 permanent operating roles.

May 20, 2026 · Energy

Brent slides to around 105 dollars as three supertankers cross the Strait of Hormuz, the first concrete sign of a partial easing of the blockade since March

Brent fell to about 105 dollars a barrel on Wednesday, down more than 5 percent on the day, on a second consecutive session of cautious optimism around the US-Iran framework and satellite imagery showing three supertankers transiting the Strait of Hormuz, the first observable easing of the March blockade. The move tightens the Basra Medium discount window after a roughly 33 dollar gap in early May, and pressures SOMO's revenue math for the rest of Q2 as Iraq leans on Ceyhan flows of around 340,000 barrels a day to substitute for the still-constrained southern terminal volumes.

Kuwait

May 20, 2026 · Governance High impact

Cabinet under PM Sheikh Ahmad sends a four-article draft decree-law to the Amir rewriting Kuwait Municipality Law 33 of 2016, swapping the elected council for a 12-member appointed body with a two-year term

Kuwait's Cabinet on Tuesday approved a four-article draft decree-law amending Law 33 of 2016 on the Kuwait Municipality, rewriting Articles 4, 6, 7, 13, 14 and 26 to install a 12-member appointed Municipal Council of Kuwaiti citizens by birth, aged 30 and above, with degrees in engineering, architecture or municipal specialisations, on a two-year term to be convened within 15 days of the appointment decree. The draft is now with Amir Sheikh Meshal for issuance, locking the municipality file under the executive while the National Assembly suspension remains in place.

May 20, 2026 · Fiscal

Brent at around 105 dollars sits well below Kuwait's 90.5 dollar break-even arithmetic on paper but tightens the gap against the budget's 57 dollar planning assumption as Q1 momentum fades on the Iran cycle

Brent's pullback to about 105 dollars on Wednesday on US-Iran framework optimism widens the gap between Kuwait's 2026-27 budget oil assumption of 57 dollars and its 90.5 dollar fiscal break-even, with spending in the new envelope set to rise 6 percent to 26 billion dinars and 76 percent of that going to salaries and subsidies, while non-oil revenue is projected up nearly 20 percent to 3.5 billion dinars on the back of fee-hike measures. Two Iranian drones hit the Komala Toilers' Banagawra site near Sulaimani, the third multi-strike incident on Iranian Kurdish opposition camps in the KRG in two weeks

Two drones launched from Iran struck a former headquarters of the Komala Toilers of Kurdistan at Banagawra in southwest Sulaimani province on Wednesday, with no injuries recorded, in the third multi-strike incident on Iranian Kurdish opposition camps in the KRG in two weeks.
