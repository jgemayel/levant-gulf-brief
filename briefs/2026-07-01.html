Syria

June 30, 2026 · Economy

Syria's 2026 budget more than triples as Damascus eyes an 8.7 billion dollar revenue haul and new mining and power authorities

Syria's finance ministry set 2026 public spending near 10.5 billion dollars, more than triple the 3.45 billion dollars budgeted for 2025, against projected revenues of about 8.7 billion dollars, a jump of roughly 149 percent, with oil and gas supplying around 28 percent. President Ahmed al-Sharaa paired the figures with three decrees standing up new mining, electricity, and water bodies to anchor the reconstruction drive.

Arab News
June 30, 2026 · Energy

Damascus runs on 24-hour electricity for the first time since 2011 as national output nears 2,400 megawatts

Syria's grid operator said generation had climbed to about 2,400 megawatts, enough to keep the capital powered around the clock for the first time in 14 years, though officials cautioned that output still covers less than a third of national demand estimated near 8 gigawatts. Stable supply beyond Damascus hinges on finishing repairs to generating units and steady deliveries of gas and fuel.

Iraq

June 30, 2026 · Security

Iraq's anti-graft raids uncover cash behind the wall as investigators seize more than 85 million dollars and call it only the first phase

Investigators pulled about 14 million dollars in cash from behind a wall at the home of arrested deputy oil minister Ali Maarij and roughly 15.5 million dollars plus gold from lawmaker Alia Nassif, part of more than 85 million dollars in assets seized across the sweep of 47 officials. Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi's office cast the crackdown as only its first phase, with parliamentary immunity lifted before the arrests.

June 29, 2026 · Energy

Iraq races to save the Kirkuk-Ceyhan line before its July 27 expiry, with al-Zaidi set for Ankara talks

With the 52-year pact governing the Kirkuk to Ceyhan pipeline due to lapse on July 27, Baghdad has stood up a negotiating committee and lined up a visit by Prime Minister al-Zaidi to Ankara to strike a replacement. Iraq is moving roughly 200,000 barrels a day through the northern route, a fraction of its more than 4 million barrels a day of export potential, as Strait of Hormuz flows recover to record levels.

Kuwait

July 1, 2026 · Energy

Kuwait's new quarterly fuel prices take effect, with Ultra 98 jumping to 275 fils a liter

Kuwait National Petroleum Company set prices for July through September that lift Ultra 98 gasoline to 275 fils a liter from 225, while Premium 91 holds at 85 fils, Super 95 at 105, and diesel and kerosene at 115. The quarterly reset follows a cabinet decision and took effect July 1.

Arab Times
June 2026 · Economy

Kuwait's investment engine picks up as oil-sector tenders advance and growth is seen near 3.8 percent

Kuwait is rolling out large energy, utilities, and transport projects faster, with a crude-pipeline lease tender among the packages advancing and investors undeterred by the wartime disruption to Strait of Hormuz shipping. The economy is seen expanding about 3.8 percent in 2026 after a two-year recession that gave way to 2.6 percent growth in 2025.

Kurdistan

June 30, 2026 · Energy

Kurdistan's oilfields come back on a phased clock, with Hunt Oil due July 8 and DNO restarting its flagship fields this week

The KRG said international operators are restoring output on a staggered timetable after repairing airstrike damage, with HKN already pumping about 7,000 barrels a day and eyeing Atrush, DNO set to restart the Tawke and Peshkabir fields this week, and Hunt Oil returning on July 8. Gulf Keystone has resumed the Shaikan field, helping regional exports climb back above 200,000 barrels a day.

June 30, 2026 · Energy

Baghdad says no obstacles remain to Kurdistan's oil exports as the Erbil deal locks in

Iraq's federal oil ministry declared that no obstacles are left to Kurdistan Region crude exports, clearing the way for volumes to rise under the Erbil-Baghdad arrangement and its agreed payment mechanism. The statement follows a joint federal-regional security review and a written guarantee to protect the fields damaged in wartime drone strikes.
