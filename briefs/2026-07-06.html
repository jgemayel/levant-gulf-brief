Syria

July 5, 2026 · Governance

Al-Sharaa decrees a national Syrian University for Security Sciences based in Damascus

President Ahmad al-Sharaa issued Decree No. 146 of 2026 on July 5 establishing the Syrian University for Security Sciences, a public institution headquartered in Damascus with authority to open affiliated colleges and centres across the provinces. The body groups a Higher Institute for Security Sciences, faculties of security sciences and cyber security, and a research centre, part of the transitional government's drive to rebuild and professionalise state security organs after the fall of Assad.

SANA
July 5, 2026 · Security

A UN inquiry and Arab states condemn the Damascus courthouse cafe bombing as the toll reaches 10

The UN Commission of Inquiry on Syria condemned the July 2 bombing of a cafe near the Palace of Justice in Damascus and called for those responsible to be held to account, as the confirmed toll rose to 10 civilians killed and 21 wounded. Lebanon, the UAE and Yemen joined the Syrian Network for Human Rights in denouncing the attack, with Damascus saying the violence would not derail its push to strengthen security and stability.

Iraq

July 5, 2026 · Energy

OPEC+ signs off on another 188,000 barrel a day rise for August, with Iraq among the seven producers

Seven OPEC+ members led by Saudi Arabia and Russia and including Iraq and Kuwait agreed at a July 5 online meeting to lift combined output by 188,000 barrels a day from August, a third consecutive monthly increase of the same size as the group unwinds the voluntary cuts set in April 2023. The decision lands as Brent has fallen back toward prewar levels and Strait of Hormuz traffic recovers under the June 17 US-Iran memorandum, with the producers set to meet again on August 2.

Al Jazeera
July 2026 · Energy

SOMO holds Iraq's northern crude exports near 190,000 barrels a day through Ceyhan

Iraq's State Oil Marketing Organization is keeping crude flows from the north stable at about 190,000 barrels a day along the pipeline to Turkey's Ceyhan port, the route that carries Kirkuk and Kurdistan barrels to the Mediterranean and that Baghdad wants to ramp far higher over the coming weeks. Federal officials have set a target of lifting pipeline shipments toward 770,000 barrels a day by mid-August as the international companies operating in Kurdistan restart fields, with the KRG-side recovery detailed in the Kurdistan section.

Kuwait

July 5, 2026 · Energy

The OPEC+ August hike hands Kuwait room to keep clawing back output wrecked by the Hormuz shutdown

Kuwait's slice of the July 5 OPEC+ decision to add 188,000 barrels a day for August gives it fresh quota headroom as it rebuilds production that collapsed to about 573,000 barrels a day in May, the steepest fall of any Gulf producer once the Strait of Hormuz crisis shut its only export route. With shipping now recovering, Kuwait Petroleum Corporation is marketing July cargoes and pushing back toward 2 million barrels a day and eventually the prewar level near 2.6 million.

July 2026 · Regulation

Kuwait tightens the rules on ride-hailing apps under a new Interior Ministry resolution

First Deputy Prime Minister and Interior Minister Sheikh Fahad Yousef Al-Sabah issued Ministerial Resolution No. 893 of 2026 setting conditions for passenger-transport and brokerage apps, requiring operators to hold a commerce licence and prior traffic-department approval and confining Kuwaiti drivers to those over 21 with clean records. The General Traffic Department can monitor the platforms and move to block any app whose operator fails to fix violations within a month of notice.

Kurdistan

July 2026 · Oil & Gas

DNO plans an eight-well drive to push Tawke and Peshkabir output toward 100,000 barrels a day

Norway's DNO, chaired by Bijan Mossavar-Rahmani, is launching an eight-well programme this year at its flagship Tawke and Peshkabir fields aimed at restoring output to about 100,000 barrels a day, replacing equipment wrecked in the drone strikes of the spring war. The push adds to a broader Kurdistan restart in which Gulf Keystone has reopened Shaikan, HKN is set to resume Atrush and Hunt Oil is due back on July 8, together targeting close to 170,000 barrels a day channelled to SOMO for export.

Kurdistan24
July 2026 · Fiscal

Barzani's cabinet presses Baghdad for the KRG's full budget share and overdue salaries as the oil deal holds

The KRG Council of Ministers under Prime Minister Masrour Barzani reaffirmed that the federal government is obliged to transfer not just public-sector salaries but the Region's full budget entitlement, framed on the 14.1 percent of Iraq's population living in Kurdistan, as ministers also weighed Peshmerga reforms. The demand rides on the revived oil-for-revenue arrangement under which Kurdish crude flows to SOMO and Baghdad is expected to release the outstanding monthly salaries.
