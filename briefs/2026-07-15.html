Syria

July 14, 2026 · Diplomacy

Trump presses Netanyahu to start pulling Israeli troops out of southern Syria

President Trump told Prime Minister Netanyahu in a July 14 call to begin redeploying Israeli forces out of Syria and to take similar steps in Lebanon, warning that the military presence in Syrian territory is stoking tensions that could tip into wider escalation. The call came a day after Trump met President Ahmad al-Sharaa on the sidelines of the NATO summit in Turkey, and al-Sharaa has kept up his demand that Israel return to the 1974 disengagement line and withdraw from the ground it took after Assad fell.

Axios
July 14, 2026 · Nuclear

Al-Sharaa hosts the IAEA chief in Damascus as cooperation on the old nuclear file deepens

President al-Sharaa received IAEA Director General Rafael Grossi and his delegation at the People's Palace on July 14, alongside Foreign Minister Asaad al-Shaibani and Atomic Energy Commission head Mudar al-Akleh, as the two sides pressed cooperation over Syria's undeclared past nuclear work and access to suspected former sites. On the same visit Grossi inaugurated a new brachytherapy unit at Damascus's al-Biruni Hospital and said the agency is moving to supply Syria with a CT scanner and a PET-CT system for cancer care.

SANA
July 14, 2026 · Energy

Baniyas re-emerges as a Mediterranean outlet in Iraq's new US-backed pipeline plan

A 4.6 billion dollar Iraqi plan to pump Basra crude north to Haditha, advanced during al-Zaidi's Washington visit, keeps Syria's port of Baniyas in play as one of three export outlets alongside Turkey's Ceyhan and Jordan's Aqaba, reviving the idea of routing Iraqi and Gulf oil to the Mediterranean through Syrian soil. For Damascus the prospect promises transit revenue and tighter integration with a US, Turkish and Gulf energy network, though any real volumes would need years of rebuilding. Iraq

July 14, 2026 · Economy

Al-Zaidi wins a raft of US energy deals at the White House as Trump promises more

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi met Trump in the Oval Office on July 14, where the president promised massive oil deals and both sides pushed to turn a security relationship into an economic one, with Chevron in talks to develop the West Qurna-2 and Nasiriyah fields, an Excelerate-led LNG import terminal, a Starlink licence and a consortium of Chevron, Capital TI and Qatar's UCC studying strategic export pipelines. Al-Zaidi pitched Baghdad as an open door for American capital and pressed for Iraq's fair share of OPEC output as the Gulf war squeezes its exports.

Al Jazeera
July 14, 2026 · Security High impact

The US reimposes its naval blockade on Iran and strikes again, keeping Iraq's Gulf artery shut

US forces reimposed a blockade on Iran's ports and coastline at 4pm Washington time on July 14 and launched a third round of strikes this week against roughly 140 Iranian military targets, after the Revolutionary Guard hit two supertankers and two UAE tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, killing one mariner. Brent settled up 1.72 percent at 84.73 dollars and touched 87 intraday, more than 10 percent above Sunday's level, while Trump dropped his demand that ships pay a 20 percent toll to cross the strait that normally carries most of Iraq's roughly 3.3 million barrels a day of exports.

Al Jazeera
July 14, 2026 · Politics

Al-Zaidi pledges militia disarmament and an end to Iranian gas imports after Pentagon talks

After meeting Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth at the Pentagon on July 14, al-Zaidi said armed factions will hand over their weapons and vowed to keep Iraqi soil from being used against neighbours, holding to a September 30 disarmament deadline. He also signalled Baghdad will not renew its deal to import Iranian gas and will lean on the Gulf electricity link and new American supply instead, a hard turn away from Tehran as he courts Washington.

Kuwait

July 14, 2026 · Security High impact

A Kuwaiti navy vessel is hit in the heaviest strike on the country since June

Kuwait's armed forces intercepted one ballistic missile, five cruise missiles and 33 drones in an Iranian barrage on July 14, but a naval vessel was struck and four sailors were wounded, spokesman Major General Saud al-Otaibi said, with falling shrapnel damaging several civilian and vital sites. It was the worst attack on Kuwait since the June airport strike, and the Defence Ministry told the public not to approach or photograph missile or drone debris and to report it on 112.

Arab Times
July 14, 2026 · Governance

Kuwait moves to fully Kuwaitize judicial support jobs by year end

The Justice Ministry announced a plan to fully Kuwaitize judicial support roles by the end of 2026, starting with court clerks, legal researchers and administrative coordinators at the Court of First Instance, Court of Appeal, Court of Cassation and Constitutional Court. As part of the first phase it will terminate the contracts of 54 non-Kuwaiti staff from November once their Kuwaiti replacements finish training, part of a broader nationalisation drive across the state.

Kurdistan

July 15, 2026 · Politics

KDP and PUK negotiators meet in Erbil to break the 20-month cabinet deadlock

Negotiating delegations from the KDP and PUK are meeting in Erbil this week to work three linked files, a unified Kurdish candidate for Iraq's presidency, the formation of the Region's tenth cabinet and reviving the Kurdistan Parliament, still stalled 20 months after the October 2024 vote that gave the KDP 39 seats to the PUK's 23. Pressure has risen since the PUK and New Generation formed the Coalition for Balance and Revival, a 38-seat bloc one short of the KDP, with the PUK pushing for the premiership or the presidency plus the oil and interior ministries.

Kurdistan24
July 14, 2026 · Energy

Kurdistan oil resumes toward 200,000 barrels a day under a new Baghdad-Erbil field-protection pact

Production is being brought back across a group of Kurdish fields with an export target near 200,000 barrels a day, after Baghdad and Erbil struck a security agreement, ordered by al-Zaidi, to shield energy sites through joint work between federal forces and the Peshmerga. The deal follows hundreds of drone and missile strikes on the Region's oil and gas infrastructure since the war reignited in late February, damage that had repeatedly knocked the fields offline.

Rudaw
July 14, 2026 · Energy

The Ceyhan route gains fresh weight as the current export protocol nears its July 27 expiry

With the Gulf blockade holding, the Region's northern lifeline to Turkey's Ceyhan matters more than ever, and Iraq and Turkey are racing to seal a one-year protocol to keep more than 200,000 barrels a day flowing before the current pipeline deal lapses on July 27. The route also anchors the Ceyhan leg of the wider Basra to Haditha export plan pushed in Washington, giving Erbil and Baghdad a way to move barrels the strait would otherwise strand.
