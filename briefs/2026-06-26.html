Syria

June 24, 2026 · Justice

Damascus opens the first public transitional-justice trial of an Assad relative as Wassim al-Assad faces the Fourth Criminal Court

The Palace of Justice in Damascus held the first public session on June 24 in the trial of Wassim Badih al-Assad, a cousin of ousted president Bashar al-Assad, before a Fourth Criminal Court newly tasked with transitional justice. Judge Fakhr al-Din al-Aryan read charges including forming two armed groups, drug smuggling and trafficking, and incitement to murder, in a case the new government is casting as a landmark test of postwar accountability. The Justice Ministry live-streamed the opening minutes before suspending the broadcast to protect witnesses.

The New Arab
June 23, 2026 · Security

Israeli ground incursions persist in southern Quneitra, with troops detaining and then releasing farmers near the Golan line

Israeli forces released a young farm worker on June 23 after detaining him during a dawn incursion into Ain Ziwan in southern Quneitra, where two tanks and roughly 30 soldiers held positions in farmland for several hours. The raids extend a run of near-daily Israeli operations across Quneitra and Daraa that Damascus says violate the 1974 disengagement accord, even as US-brokered security contacts continue.

Iraq

June 25, 2026 · Energy High impact

Iraq warns it could leave OPEC unless its production quota is raised, piling pressure on the bloc after the UAE's exit

Oil Ministry spokesman Salim al-Rikabi said on June 25 that Iraq has no current plan to withdraw from OPEC but will weigh staying or exiting if the group does not lift its quota to match the country's capacity and revenue needs. The threat from OPEC's second-largest producer lands with Brent near 74 dollars, back at pre-war levels as Strait of Hormuz traffic normalizes, and follows the UAE's May 1 departure over the same quota grievance. The squeeze on rebuilt exports into a falling price is shared by Kuwait in the next section.

June 25, 2026 · Economy

Al-Zaidi says US companies will be a top priority in Iraq as he readies a mid-July White House visit

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi said on June 25 that American firms would be a top priority for investment and energy contracts in Iraq, framing a deeper US commercial role as central to his agenda alongside a pledge to bring weapons under state control. The businessman premier, in office since May, is preparing to present a completed cabinet before his planned meeting with President Trump in mid-July.

The National
June 2026 · Energy

Iraq heads into peak summer with its power grid badly weakened, as gas shortfalls deepen blackout hours

Iraq is entering the hottest weeks of the year with generation strained by falling associated gas output, unfinished import infrastructure, and the volatility of Iranian supply after the war, raising the risk of long daily blackouts and the unrest they tend to trigger in the south. Gas shortages remain the single biggest constraint on supply hours, leaving central and southern provinces exposed just as demand peaks.

Kuwait

June 24, 2026 · Economy

Kuwait backs a Saudi call to review the OPEC Fund's governance as Finance Minister Al-Refai leads the Vienna delegation

Finance Minister Yaqoub al-Refai headed Kuwait's delegation to the fifth OPEC Fund for International Development Forum in Vienna, where Kuwait supported a Saudi proposal for an independent review of the Board of Governors' structure and working mechanisms. On the sidelines, al-Refai discussed expanding capacity-building and technical-exchange cooperation with OPEC Fund president Abdulhamid Alkhalifa, as the forum focused on development financing, energy and food security.

Kurdistan

June 24, 2026 · Politics

Masrour Barzani says there is no justification for delaying the Kurdistan parliament's reactivation

Speaking at a park inauguration in Erbil on June 24, KRG Prime Minister Masrour Barzani made his most forceful call yet to revive the Kurdistan Region's legislature, saying there is no excuse to obstruct its return and that any party blocking the process must answer to the people. The chamber has stayed idle roughly 20 months after elections, with the KDP and PUK still at odds over forming a new cabinet.

June 24, 2026 · Security

Baghdad pledges air defenses and Peshmerga deployments to shield Kurdistan's oil fields after wartime drone strikes

Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi has promised security guarantees for the international firms operating Kurdistan's oil fields, with Erbil and Baghdad agreeing to install air-defense systems and keep trained Peshmerga units at the sites to fend off drone and missile attacks. The arrangement follows a wave of strikes on northern energy infrastructure, many blamed on Iran-aligned militias, that forced repeated shutdowns and threatened the oil revenue underpinning the region's salaries.
