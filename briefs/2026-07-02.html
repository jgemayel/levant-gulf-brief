Syria

July 2, 2026 · Aviation

Syrian Airlines flies its first European route in over a decade as the Damascus to Amsterdam service launches

The national carrier operated its first scheduled flight to a European city in more than 13 years on July 2, restoring the Damascus to Amsterdam link that the civil aviation authority casts as the opening leg of a broader return to Europe. Officials said talks with German authorities on lifting the ban on Syria to Germany flights are expected to conclude in the first half of July, part of a wave that has brought at least 12 airlines back to Damascus and Aleppo since the Assad government fell in December 2024.

Iraq

July 2, 2026 · Politics

Iraq's graft purge widens as parliament strips accused MPs of immunity and a lawmaker is found with 15 million dollars in cash

Speaker Haybat al-Halbousi lifted the parliamentary immunity of lawmakers named in the corruption case, using his authority to act while parliament is on legislative leave, and arrest warrants were then executed. State media put the named suspects at 15, including 12 sitting MPs, a former legislator, and an ex-adviser to former premier Mohammed Shia al-Sudani, with lawmaker Alia Nassif alone found holding some 15 million dollars in cash at her home as Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi calls the sweep only a first phase.

Associated Press
July 2026 · Security

After the graft sweep, al-Zaidi signals armed factions are the next front in his push to restore state authority

Fresh off detaining lawmakers and officials, the prime minister is framing corruption and arms held outside state control as two halves of the same sovereignty problem, with reporting pointing to armed factions as the next target of his campaign to consolidate the state's monopoly on force. The signal lands as al-Zaidi prepares a mid-July Washington visit built around an economic and security partnership, and as Iran-linked lawmakers press to revive a stalled law formalizing the Popular Mobilization Forces.

Kuwait

July 1, 2026 · Aviation

Kuwait Airways sets a July 15 return to Damascus, its first flights to Syria in 14 years

Syria's civil aviation authority said Kuwait Airways will begin regular direct service between Kuwait City and Damascus on July 15, resuming a route suspended for 14 years and adding the Gulf carrier to a growing roster of airlines returning to Syrian skies. The move tracks the same reopening that has Syrian Airlines flying to Europe again, tightening Kuwait to Damascus links as postwar traffic rebuilds.

Kurdistan

July 1, 2026 · Politics

Kurdistan's parliament slips into a two-month summer recess after just one session in 20 months

The legislature began a two-month recess on July 1 having convened only once since the October 2024 election, as the formation of the Region's tenth cabinet stays deadlocked between the KDP, which holds 39 of the 100 seats, and the PUK, which holds 23. Prime Minister Masrour Barzani insists reactivating parliament is the first essential step to breaking the impasse, but the break leaves government formation frozen deep into the summer.

Kurdistan24
July 1, 2026 · Economy

An Erbil delegation presses Baghdad for its full budget share as the salary-for-revenue deal comes under strain

A KRG delegation headed by council of ministers Diwan chief Omed Sabah met federal officials in Baghdad on July 1 to demand its budget allocation be set by the late-2024 census, which puts the Region at 14.1 percent of Iraq's population, rather than being limited to public-sector salaries. Talks also covered the mechanism for the Region's 120 billion dinar monthly transfer of non-oil revenue to Baghdad, the counterpart to regular salary payments under the standing agreement.
